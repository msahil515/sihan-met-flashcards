Why the mock is the whole plan

Confusion comes from not knowing what you don't know. A real past-paper mock, taken cold with no revision first, answers that in one sitting. Your score plus every flagged question is your "what I don't know" map. From then on you only study your misses, weakest area first, and you never re-read what you already got right. That's the difference between six panicked days and six focused ones.

    Exam-day rules

    Decided now, not on the day
    Two passes
    Pass one: answer everything you know cold, flag the rest. Pass two: spend the leftover time only on the flagged ones. Never read all 100 in order once.
    Skip rule
    If a question runs long on the first pass, flag it and move. Time kills the score, not the hard question. Come back on pass two.
    Walk in calm
    By the 11th you've already sat this paper twice and studied your exact holes. Nothing on it is new. Skim the trap log in the morning, nothing else goes in.
