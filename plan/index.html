    Exam-day rules

    Decided in advance, not on the day
    Skip rule
    If a Q takes >90 sec on first pass, flag and move on. Come back at the end. Time, not knowledge, kills your score.
    Guess rule (MET +4 / −1)
    Guess only when you can eliminate at least 2 of 4. Blind guess = −1 expectancy. Eliminated-2 guess = +0.5 expectancy.
    NIMHANS +1 / −0.25
    Negative marking is gentler. Guess if you can eliminate even one option (+0.5 expectancy). Don't leave Q blank if you have any signal.
