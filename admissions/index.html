SGT University — entrance syllabus & pattern (folded in 4 Jul 2026)

Straight answer first: SGT does not publish a standalone syllabus PDF like RML's CET-166. I checked the official MA Clinical Psychology (RCI) course page, the admission-procedure page and the live admission portal — none of them carry a topic list. The official wording is that the entrance "syllabus will be as prescribed by SGT University as per the guidelines of the UGC." In plain terms that means it's a general psychology aptitude test drawing on the standard PG-psychology core, not a new syllabus you have to prep from scratch. Anything below tagged official is off SGT's own pages; the topic map is my read of what a UGC-guideline general-psych test pulls from, not a published SGT list.
