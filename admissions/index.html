What to do, in order

1. RML (GGSIPU portal) — closes 7 June, do this today. Earliest deadline, runs on its own CET-166 entrance so it does not depend on CUET-PG. ₹2,500, 70% test + 30% interview. Get the form in first.

2. NFSU Gandhinagar + NFSU Delhi — both due 10 June. Both RCI, both selected on your CUET-PG 2026 score (no separate NFSU test). Two shots off the one Samarth portal.

3. The CUET-PG question. NFSU shortlists on CUET-PG, so the real question is: did you sit CUET-PG 2026 and is your score in? If yes, submit both NFSU forms. If you did not write CUET-PG, NFSU can’t shortlist you, so RML (own entrance) is your main live shot and the 2027 cycle is the bigger play.

4. Quick portal-check CSMU / CSJMU / Ashtavakra for an extension, but treat them as closed.
  • DIMHANS, Dharwad is the standout on paper: closest govt option (~230 km), own psychiatric hospital so clinical hours are in-house, same −0.25 mock scale as NIMHANS. Two gates: Karnataka domicile is mandatory (Udupi counts), and the M.Phil track runs only 4 seats. Watch the portal from ~September.
  • Christ correction: the earlier "Delhi NCR open till June 14" was wrong — Christ’s RCI MA closed 2 February 2026. Any "June" Christ date you see is a different non-RCI stream; check the RCI tag before applying.
  • 2027 cycle: NIMHANS, IHBAS, CIP and RINPAS typically open applications between December 2026 and February 2027, entrances around April–May. That’s your runway for the top RCI seats.
  • Amity (Jaipur + Noida) are open now on a rolling cycle, so they’re in the live group, but two warnings: (1) confirm in writing it’s the RCI-regulated MA, not the regular non-licensing one, and confirm that RCI tag directly with RCI; (2) the entrance question count, marking scheme, exact interview format and seat count are not published for MA Clinical, so they’re left unknown here.
