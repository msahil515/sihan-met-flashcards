What to prioritise

1. RML (GGSIPU portal) — closes 7 June, do this today. It's the earliest deadline and it runs on its own CET-166 entrance, so it does not depend on CUET-PG. ₹2,500 fee, 70% test + 30% interview. Get the form in first.

2. NFSU Gandhinagar + NFSU Delhi — both due 10 June, both RCI, both ride your CUET-PG score. Get both in off the single Samarth portal.

3. The CUET-PG question. Because NFSU selects on CUET-PG (not its own test), the real action item is: did you sit CUET-PG 2026, and is your score in? If yes, submit both NFSU forms. If you did not write CUET-PG, NFSU can't shortlist you, so RML (own entrance) becomes your main live shot and the 2027 cycle (NIMHANS/CIP/IHBAS open Dec 2026–Feb 2027) is the bigger play.

4. Quick portal-check CSJMU / CSMU / Ashtavakra in case of an extension, but treat them as closed.

Before you pay

Every date here was cross-checked on 2 June 2026 but admission portals move dates, extend windows, and reuse last year's pages. For any program you actually apply to: open the institute's own portal, confirm the RCI tag, confirm the current deadline, and confirm the fee before paying. Aggregator sites (including the ones linked here) lag the official notices.
