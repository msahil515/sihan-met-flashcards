Read this first, it changes the whole map. From 2026 the old M.Phil Clinical Psychology is being replaced by the MA Clinical Psychology (RCI) under NEP 2020 + RCI restructuring. So the new RCI-tagged MA at NIMHANS / CIP / IHBAS / RINPAS / NFSU is the licensing program now, it's not a lesser stepping stone. What does NOT license you is a plain academic MA/MSc Clinical Psychology with no RCI tag (Amity, most private universities). The only thing that matters: does it carry the RCI tag.

What to prioritise

1. NFSU Gandhinagar + NFSU Delhi — both due 10 June, both RCI, both ride your CUET-PG score. This is your whole live game. Get both in off the single Samarth portal.

2. The CUET-PG question. Because NFSU selects on CUET-PG (not its own test), the real action item is: did you sit CUET-PG 2026, and is your score in? If yes, just submit both NFSU forms. If you did not write CUET-PG, NFSU can't shortlist you and the two NFSU lines effectively don't apply, in which case 2026 RCI MA seats are basically gone and the play is to target the 2027 cycle (NIMHANS/CIP/IHBAS open Dec 2026–Feb 2027). Confirm your CUET-PG status before anything else.

3. Quick portal-check CSJMU / CSMU / ABVIMS-RML in case of an extension, but treat them as closed.

Before you pay

Every date here was cross-checked on 2 June 2026 but admission portals move dates, extend windows, and reuse last year's pages. For any program you actually apply to: open the institute's own portal, confirm the RCI tag, confirm the current deadline, and confirm the fee before paying. Aggregator sites (including the ones linked here) lag the official notices.

Sources: UPS Education latest psychology admissions, NFSU Delhi, NFSU Gandhinagar, Christ, NIMHANS, IHBAS, and institute portals (NFSU Samarth, NIMHANS, Christ, CIP Ranchi). RCI = Rehabilitation Council of India, the body that licenses clinical psychologists.
