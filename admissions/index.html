Why so many “unknown” cells: the RCI approved-institutions list is a recognition register, not an admissions brochure. It proves a college is allowed to run the program and how many seats RCI sanctioned, but it never prints fees, opening/closing dates or a portal link. Filling those from third-party aggregators is exactly the guessing you told me to stop. Pick the colleges you want from this verified list and I’ll open each official portal one by one to fill its dates and fees for real.
