IHBAS vs RML — what actually carries across

Built 3 Aug 2026 · off your RML 2020 attempt (91 right, 9 wrong, 364/400) and the 292 real IHBAS questions already tagged on this site

You said RML topics are very different from IHBAS. Half right, and the half that is wrong is the expensive half. This page does not argue about it. It puts the official IHBAS blueprint, the 292 tagged questions from three real IHBAS papers, and the real section split of your RML 2020 attempt in the same table and lets the counts answer.

Three corrections to what I told you on WhatsApp

1. Wrong: “IHBAS is the six-area general psych paper, roughly 10 each, and it is not DSM heavy.”
Right: the official IHBAS blueprint is Clinical Psychology 25 + Abnormal Psychology 15 = 40 of 100, then six areas at 10 each for the remaining 60. All 292 questions across IHBAS 2020, 2024 and 2025 tag out at 33–38 for that clinical + abnormal block every single year. IHBAS is not the less clinical paper of the two. On the counts below it is the more clinical one.

2. Wrong: “RML, longer paper, 2.5 hrs.”
Right: 2 hours, 100 MCQs × 4 = 400 marks, no negative. That is still longer per question than IHBAS (72 s vs 54 s), but not by the margin I gave you.

3. Wrong: “the social work strand cost you 3 Qs.”
Right: four of the nine. Q4 and Q18 are social-work history, Q32 (case management) and Q82 (group properties) are case-work systems. Same lineage, one gap, and it is the largest single cluster in your nine.

And one thing I did not say at all. The paper you sat is the RML M.Phil CET. CET-166, the MA you actually applied to, is a different paper: 70% test + 30% interview, with a Section A of general awareness, logical reasoning and research aptitude (~20 Q) that the M.Phil paper does not have. So RML 2020 is a fair content rehearsal and an unreliable format rehearsal. Details on the RML brief.

The claim that does not survive the table

“RML leans clinical, IHBAS is the general psych paper.” Add up the clinical block on each:

39 against 36. They are the same paper on the thing you thought separated them. The difference is not how much clinical, it is which half: RML 2020 put 19 into abnormal and only 8 into therapy, IHBAS splits it 17 / 15. So the part of the clinical block that genuinely changes when you move from RML to IHBAS is psychotherapy and the therapy schools — who founded what, named in full, roughly twice as often.

And the split moves year to year even inside IHBAS. Abnormal ran 11 → 13 → 27 across IHBAS 2020, 2024, 2025 while clinical/therapy ran 22 → 10 → 11. The block is stable at 33–38. Which half carries it is not predictable, so prepare both halves as one unit and never bet on last year's split.

What is RML-only — do not file it as psychology revision

  • Social work history. Q4 (who studied the effectiveness of social casework) and Q18 (first school of social work in India). Richmond, TISS, Delhi School of Social Work. Named-fact recall, not psychology.
  • Case work and group work systems. Q32 (desired outcomes of case management) and Q82 (common group properties). Both EXCEPT-framed, both from the casework literature.
  • Section A of CET-166 — general awareness, logical reasoning, research aptitude, ~20 questions. The M.Phil paper you sat has none of it, and IHBAS has none of it either.

Do not drop any of it. That is still around 12 marks on the RML M.Phil paper and a whole section on CET-166. The point is only that it earns you nothing on IHBAS, CIP, JSS or DIMHANS, so it belongs in an RML box, not in the six-areas rotation. Everything on it is already written up in the social-work primer.

What IHBAS will hit that RML 2020 barely touched

  • Psychotherapy and the therapy schools — 15 vs 8. Roughly double. Who founded which therapy, with the theorist named in full, plus the mechanism. This is the single biggest content shift between the two papers.
  • Personality — 10 vs 0. Not a weakness, an unknown. You have no score on it from this paper at all. Fastest area to make testable: the 119-question Systems & Theories bank already exists.
  • Biopsychology — 13 vs 9, and you are at 78%. Heavier weight on top of your worst carried accuracy. This is where the reweighting says you lose the most marks (2.9), more than any other area.
  • Speed — 54 s vs 72 s per question. IHBAS gives you 25% less time per question than the RML paper did, with no negative marking, so the correct strategy is different: never leave a blank, and never spend a third minute anywhere.

What to do with this

  • Biopsych first. Biggest reweighted leak, heavier on IHBAS than on RML, and it is fixed content that closes permanently. Q17 (long-term adiposity signalling) and Q47 (aphasia as the umbrella term) were both straight recall.
  • Then therapy schools and personality, because together they are 25 of the IHBAS 100 and RML 2020 gave you evidence on only 8 of those 25.
  • Leave stats, developmental and assessment alone. 100% on all three, and they carry across all four colleges. Re-drilling them is the most expensive way to feel productive.
  • Keep the social-work material, in its own box. RML only. It does not enter the six-areas rotation.
