Name Lock

Every named thing an MCQ can ask about: effects, biases, phenomena, experiments, laws and terms. Each card has the name, who did it and when, what it means in plain words, and the lookalike it gets confused with. Built off every PYQ on the site, wrong options included, and anything you miss in a mock lands in My misses on its own.

Pick what to drill:

20 cards a round. Spaced repetition: a miss comes back next round, a hit waits 1, then 3, 7, 16, 35 days. Cards due first, then your misses, then new ones.

Two names that trap you, side by side. Read the description, pick which one it is, then read the tell that separates them.

Two feeds land here: any card you get wrong in a drill, and any mock or PYQ question you got wrong on this device (or a synced one) whose question or options name a card. It refreshes every time you open the page. Get a card right twice in a row in a drill and it leaves the list.

Terms split into their roots, then the plain meaning and the lookalike. Learn the roots once and a term you have never seen can be decoded in the exam hall.
