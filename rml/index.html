Read this first — which RML program is CET-166. RML runs two RCI clinical-psych trainings and they get mixed up constantly:
• the legacy M.Phil Clinical Psychology (2-yr, ~13 seats, the old gold-standard licensing route, very competitive, its CET is 100 MCQs × 4 marks, no negative, 90% CET + 10% interview), and
• the new MA Clinical Psychology (RCI) — this is CET-166, the one you applied to. From 2025-26 the RCI MA replaces the M.Phil as the entry licensing qualification, so this MA is the program that leads to a Clinical Psychologist registration. Its written/interview split is reported as 70% test + 30% interview (not the M.Phil's 90/10).
Both are at the same hospital, both through GGSIPU, both RCI. The numbers below are for CET-166, the MA. Where a figure only exists for the M.Phil, it's labelled as such. 1 · The full syllabus + a prep checklist

GGSIPU doesn't publish a tight, numbered CET-166 syllabus the way NIMHANS does. What's confirmed is the list of areas the paper draws on; the depth comes from how RML/IPU clinical papers have actually behaved (section 2). Here's the area list turned into a checklist you can work from — and the good news up top:

Almost none of this is new for you. The CET-166 subject syllabus is the same nine-area psychology core you've been grinding for MET/NIMHANS. The cheatsheets already on this site are your RML prep — they're cross-linked in the right column below. The only RML-specific tilts are: lean harder on Indian Psychology, treat abnormal as factual/ICD-deep (not just DSM), and add a thin general-awareness / reasoning / research-aptitude layer for Section A.

Section A — General Awareness, Logical Reasoning & Research Aptitude ~20 marks

  • Logical / analytical reasoning: series, analogies, syllogisms, odd-one-out, basic data interpretation. Standard CET-style aptitude. A couple of timed mock sets is enough — you don't need a quant course.
  • Research aptitude: this overlaps your strong area. Hypotheses (null/alternative, directional), variables, sampling (probability vs non-probability), reliability & validity types, design (experimental / quasi / correlational), ethics. → already covered cold in Biostats + Research Methods.
  • General awareness: mental-health / psychology current affairs — RCI & its Acts, NMHP, Mental Healthcare Act 2017, DMHP, big institutes (NIMHANS, IHBAS, RML), landmark Indian psychologists. Light, factual, recognise-not-recall.

Section B — Subject-Oriented Psychology ~50 marks · the bulk of your score

2 · The depth it actually goes to how the paper behaves

The honest situation: CET-166 (the MA) is brand new — its first cycle is 2025-26, so there's no bank of released MA papers yet. But the M.Phil/IPU clinical papers at RML are well-documented, and prep-community write-ups describe exactly how RML's clinical entrance behaves. The MA draws on the same question-bank style. Here's the real depth, from those sources:

Clinical & therapy = factual, founder-driven.Past RML clinical papers are "mostly factual questions about the therapies — who developed which therapy." Plus specific therapy terms and the abbreviations they use (REBT, DBT, ACT, EMDR, CBT, IPT, MI…). So drill the founder pairs and the acronyms, not just the concepts.Trap: knowing what CBT is but blanking on who (Beck) vs REBT (Ellis), or what ACT/DBT/MI stand for. The paper rewards the name + acronym, not the vibe.
Abnormal = in-depth, and deliberately beyond DSM.RML abnormal questions are in-depth and factual, and they specifically like disorders that aren't in current DSM — older nosology and ICD entries. So don't prep DSM-5 alone; know the ICD-11 side and the classic syndromes/eponyms textbooks still carry.Trap: a DSM-only revision misses exactly the items they fish for. Fish's Clinical Psychopathology (it's in your Drive library) is the ICD-flavoured source for these.
Indian Psychology = history AND the present.They ask Indian psychology "past and present" — the history of psychology in India (very important) and the new developments. So both ends: who set up the first departments/labs, the classical frameworks (gunas/koshas/Gita), and the current shape (RCI, institutes, recent shifts).Trap: revising only the ancient/Vedantic material and skipping the modern Indian-psych history (and vice-versa). They test the timeline end to end.
The five domains that carry the paper.Prep-community consensus on RML/IHBAS-style clinical entrances: concentrate on Statistics, Research Methodology, Social Psychology, Clinical Psychology, and Personality & Developmental Psychology. That's where the marks live.Trap: over-investing in niche biopsych minutiae while leaving stats/research-methods thin — those two are pure points and you're already strong there.
Format is set-based MCQ, multiple booklets.RML/IHBAS-style clinical CETs run as multiple shuffled sets (A/B/C/D) of MCQs. The M.Phil paper is 100 MCQs × 4 (no negative). The MA's exact count isn't confirmed and MA papers can include a descriptive part — but expect a large MCQ block, no negative marking, so attempt everything.Trap: leaving MCQs blank "to be safe." With no negative marking, a blank is a wasted point — mark your best guess on every question.
You already have RML's real papers. Six previous-year RML papers (2020-25) are sitting in your Mimaansa Drive Library, pinned at the top. Those are the single best depth-calibration you can get — sit one like a mock, then come back and the weak areas it exposes map straight onto the cheatsheets above. The cleanest one is now built as a native mock: RML 2024 PYQ Mock → — all 100 questions verbatim, the paper's official answer key, untimed Practice mode by default (instant reveal + explanation), with a timed Exam mode if you want it.

3 · What the forums say the paper & program are like

"How do people actually crack it?"NCERT base + PYQs + mocks. The repeated line across prep forums: build the base from standard texts/NCERT, then grind previous-year papers and mock tests — the RML/IPU clinical paper rewards recognition of repeated factual items, so PYQ exposure beats reading new material. You're already running mocks; point them at the five domains above.
"Are the papers even available?"Not officially. RML/IHBAS don't release papers from the centre; the circulating PYQs are collected from students who sat the exam (that's why your Mimaansa Drive set is valuable — it's exactly that). Treat any "official RML PYQ PDF" online as student-reconstructed, useful but not gospel on exact wording.
"How competitive is RML really?"The M.Phil is brutal — ~13 seats, one of the most sought-after clinical entrances in the country, so the cutoff is high and the paper is dense. The MA (CET-166) is newer and a wider gate, but it's still RML/Delhi and RCI-licensing, so expect strong competition. Honest read: the MA is your realistic, licensing-valid shot at RML without going through the M.Phil meat-grinder. Don't underestimate it, don't be scared off it.
The thing the community actually stressesSame licensing logic as Amity: a plain academic MA does not register you with RCI. CET-166 is the RCI-recognised one, which is the whole point of doing RML over a generic MA — it's a licence-track program at a top government teaching hospital. That's the value here. Just keep the confirmation in writing when you're admitted.

How to attack it (and the interview)

You applied today, the paper's the next thing. Here's the plan, weighted to how the paper behaves:

  1. Sit one RML PYQ from your Drive set as a cold mock (Library, RML papers pinned). It calibrates the real depth faster than anything else and tells you which cheatsheets to re-hit.
  2. Lock the five domains: Stats + Research Methods, Social, Clinical/Abnormal, Personality + Developmental. That's where the marks are.
  3. Drill founder-pairs + acronyms in Psychotherapy — RML asks "who developed which therapy" and the abbreviations directly. Make these reflexive.
  4. Over-prepare Indian Psychology, both ends — modern history of Indian psych and the classical frameworks. Use the Amity Indian-psych block; it's the same load and your recurring leak.
  5. Go ICD-deep on abnormal, not DSM-only. The disorders DSM dropped are exactly what RML fishes for; Fish's Psychopathology (in your Drive) is the source.
  6. Thin Section-A pass: two timed reasoning sets + skim mental-health current affairs (RCI, MHCA 2017, NMHP, big institutes). Don't over-invest, it's ~20 marks.
  7. No blanks. No negative marking → mark a best guess on every question.

The interview (30% — don't sleep on it)

At 30% of your final score, the RML PI carries real weight (more than the M.Phil's 10%). It shares almost everything with your Amity interview prep and your dissertation prep sheet — reuse both. What RML-style clinical PIs probe:

AreaWhat a strong answer does
Why clinical psychologySpecific to clinical work (assessment, psychopathology, therapy with real patients in a hospital setting), tied to the RCI/licence path — not "I like helping people."
Your dissertationThey'll grill your own research — you've got the full prep sheet for this (design, why each scale, why Spearman/logistic, findings, limitations). Know it cold.
Core knowledge liveClean answers on DSM/ICD, a therapy school, a stats concept. The same Section-B content, spoken.
Fit for a hospital trainingShow you understand RML is a busy government teaching hospital — clinical load, supervision, real patients. Signal you want that, not just a degree.
Ethics / awarenessConfidentiality, scope of practice, one current mental-health topic ready.
