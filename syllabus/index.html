What the real papers actually did FROM PAST PAPERS

Rather than trust the blueprint on its own, every question of the three IHBAS papers already on this site — 2020 (92 Q), 2024 (100 Q, official key) and 2025 (100 Q) — was tagged by area and counted. 292 real questions. The blueprint holds up. Clinical + assessment lands at 22 against the promised 25; abnormal at 17 against 15; stats, social and developmental all sit within a question or two of their 10. The one visible difference is "General", which reads 18 instead of 10 — because personality items get tagged into general here, and the blueprint's separate Personality-10 accounts for almost exactly the gap. In other words: three independent real papers, and no section of the published blueprint is fake.
What moves year to year: abnormal swung 11 → 13 → 27 and clinical/therapy swung 22 → 10 → 11. The clinical-plus-abnormal block is stable at roughly 33–38 questions, but which half of it carries the weight is not predictable. Prepare both as one unit; don't bet on last year's split.

What to actually study

  • Clinical + abnormal (≈35–40 Q) — ICD-10/DSM-5 criteria, first-rank symptoms, differential diagnosis, therapy schools and who founded them. Notes app · IHBAS 2025 mock · IHBAS 2024 · IHBAS 2020
  • General / cognitive / learning + personality (≈18–20 Q) — schools and systems, conditioning, memory, perception, personality theories with the theorist named. General psych
  • Biopsychology (≈13 Q) — pathways, lobes, neurotransmitters, endocrine. Biopsych cheatsheet
  • Social (≈11 Q) — attitudes, attribution, conformity, aggression.
  • Stats & research methods (≈10 Q) — designs, sampling, reliability/validity, basic tests. Biostats cheatsheet
  • Developmental (≈9 Q) and assessment (≈7 Q) — stage theories; the standard test battery.
No negative marking, 90 minutes for 100 questions. Leave nothing blank, ever. That's roughly 54 seconds a question, so speed is a real constraint — the 2024 and 2025 mocks on this site are the right rehearsal.

CIP Ranchi

Central Institute of Psychiatry, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare · M.Phil Clinical Psychology (Stream A)

Scheme of examination OFFICIAL

"There shall be an online Computer Based Test consisting of 100 MCQs type questions to be answered in 60 minutes. ONE mark will be given for the correct response. No marks would be deducted for the wrong answer. The shortlisted candidates shall be called for practical/interview. The Computer Based Test (CBT) will have questions of MA/M.Sc. standard. The Computer Based Test holds 100 marks and Practical/Interview holds 50 marks."
— CIP Prospectus for Stream A (M.Phil & Ph.D. Clinical Psychology), verbatim

That's the entire published "syllabus": the words "MA/M.Sc. standard". CIP has never released a topic list. Anyone selling you a "CIP syllabus PDF" made it up. Also note the exam is 60 minutes for 100 questions — 36 seconds a question, the tightest of the four by a distance.

Read this honestly: it's one paper. The coaching write-ups all list social psychology as a "dominating domain" at CIP; the 2020 paper had exactly one social item. Either the coaching claim is padding, or the weighting genuinely moves year to year. Two conclusions that are safe from a single paper: (1) general + abnormal is over half the paper, and (2) CIP asks a lot of pure-recall history and "who coined the term" items — Kraepelin, Bleuler, Cullen, Witmer, Braid, Reil, Sengupta, Kinsey, Holmes & Rahe all showed up. That's an easy, cheap block of marks and it maps straight onto the Systems & Theories book.
To lock this down I need CIP 2023 too. With two papers the weighting stops being a guess. CIP 2020 is already in hand; 2023 was on the earlier list of papers you mentioned. Send it and this section gets rebuilt off two papers, plus a full CIP mock like the IHBAS ones.

Where CIP differs from IHBAS in practice

  • Interview is a third of your total. 100 CBT + 50 practical/interview. IHBAS has no interview at all. Your therapy-style profile and dissertation prep are worth real marks here, not just IHBAS-style question drill.
  • 36 seconds a question. Practise at speed, not at leisure.
  • Almost no therapy questions in 2020 — the clinical weight sat in psychopathology, not treatment. Opposite of IHBAS 2020.

DIMHANS Dharwad

Dharwad Institute of Mental Health & Neurosciences · Government of Karnataka · affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Bengaluru · mentored by NIMHANS

Read this first — it may end the question. Both DIMHANS clinical-psychology courses require a Karnataka domicile certificate (or a 7-year Karnataka residence certificate). It is a state-government institute filling seats on the Karnataka reservation roster. Unless you hold Karnataka domicile, no amount of syllabus prep gets you a seat. Confirm this against the current notification before you write DIMHANS off or spend a week on it — but don't plan around it until you've confirmed it.

You didn't say which course, so both are here. They are very different exams.

Syllabus GAP

DIMHANS publishes no syllabus and no topic list for either course, and unlike IHBAS and CIP there is no DIMHANS past paper in your collection — nothing in the Drive library, nothing in the 76-page pack, nothing in the WhatsApp uploads. So there is nothing honest to reconstruct from. Two things would fix that:

  • A DIMHANS past paper or memory-based recall from anyone who has sat it — then it gets the same treatment as CIP 2020 above.
  • The current official notification PDF. The DIMHANS site (dimhans.karnataka.gov.in) refuses connections from outside India, so it couldn't be opened from here. It opens fine on an Indian connection — if you load it and send the notification PDF, this section gets filled properly.
Interim working answer: the M.Phil entrance is an RGUHS-affiliated, NIMHANS-mentored, 100-question PG psychology paper. Functionally that is the same core as IHBAS and CIP, with a negative-marking penalty bolted on. Prep the same seven areas; the only behavioural change is don't blind-guess, because −0.25 makes a coin flip on a 4-option question break roughly even at best.
