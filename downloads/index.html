How to load on Kindle

Two routes, pick whichever is faster
Send to Kindle, email
Email the EPUB to your Send to Kindle address from a verified email account (find it in Manage Your Content and Devices). Amazon converts to KF8 and pushes to your library. Usually arrives in under 5 minutes.
Sideload via USB
Plug the Kindle in, drop the .epub into documents/ on the device, eject. Newer Kindles support EPUB natively; older models will still take it via the Send-to-Kindle desktop app.
