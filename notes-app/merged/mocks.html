Vision quick reference

Cones are most concentrated at the fovea centralis (you picked "peripheral retina"). Fovea = almost pure cones, sharpest acuity + colour; rods dominate the periphery; the optic disc has neither (= blind spot).

  • Rods: ~120 million, peripheral, low light, no colour, rhodopsin. Cones: ~6 million, foveal, bright light, colour (S/M/L = blue/green/red).
  • Trichromatic theory (Young-Helmholtz) = receptor level. Opponent-process (Hering) = ganglion-cell level (red-green, blue-yellow, black-white).
  • Lateral inhibition (horizontal/amacrine cells) → edge enhancement (Mach bands).
  • Pathway: retina → optic nerve → chiasm → optic tract → LGN (thalamus) → V1 (BA 17, calcarine sulcus). Dorsal stream (where/how, parietal) vs ventral stream (what, temporal → fusiform face area).

The amino-acid → neurotransmitter chain (PKU sits at step 1)

LTP and the NMDA receptor

Long-term potentiation = persistent synaptic strengthening with repeated stimulation (Bliss & Lomo, 1973, hippocampus). The NMDA receptor is the coincidence detector: needs both glutamate binding AND postsynaptic depolarisation (to expel the Mg²⁺ block) → Ca²⁺ floods in → more AMPA receptors inserted → synapse permanently stronger. AMPA = fast excitatory transmission; NMDA = the plasticity gate-keeper. Memorise the chain: hippocampus + NMDA + Ca²⁺ + LTP = cellular substrate of learning/memory.

Auditory place vs frequency theory

Georg von Békésy (Nobel 1961). Place theory: pitch encoded by location of maximum basilar-membrane displacement — high frequencies vibrate the basal (narrow, stiff) end, low frequencies the apical (wide, floppy) end. Best above ~1 kHz. Frequency/volley theory (Wever): low frequencies encoded by neurons firing in volleys phase-locked to the wave. Above ~5 kHz, place theory does all the work.

Wernicke-Korsakoff: one disease, two phases

You got Korsakoff questions right but flagged "what is korsakoff, need to learn this." Cause = thiamine (B1) deficiency, classically chronic alcoholism (poor diet + reduced absorption); also bariatric surgery, hyperemesis, malnutrition.

p-values & error types ("knew it well before, need revision")

p-value = probability of the observed data (or more extreme), assuming H₀ is true. It is NOT the probability H₀ is true, NOT the probability you're wrong, NOT the effect size. p < α → reject H₀ ("statistically significant"); p ≥ α → fail to reject (never "accept H₀"). α is set before the study.
