PYQ order

Which past paper to sit first, second, third, and why that order. Built 26 July 2026. Your live set is IHBAS, CIP, JSS, DIMHANS, so everything is weighted toward IHBAS and CIP first, then the closed cycles as volume. Tick a step off when you've finished it and read every explanation on it; the ticks save on this device.

Two rules for the whole run

1. Don't move to the next paper until you've read every explanation on the one you just did. Including the ones you got right by guessing. A guessed right answer is a miss you didn't get charged for. Every mock on the site shows the full explanation plus a trap line under each wrong option, so this is reading, not hunting.

2. IHBAS has no negative marking. On the three IHBAS papers you attempt all 100, never leave a blank. The site mocks are all scored +1 / −0.25 so they stay comparable to each other, so your on-page score will read lower than the real exam would. Ignore the penalty on IHBAS papers and count attempted-and-right out of 100.

What that adds up to

Seven steps, eleven papers, about 1,190 questions, and all but one of them is a real exam somebody actually sat. At one paper a day with the explanations properly read, that's roughly a fortnight of work. Do not compress it. The reading is the part that moves your score.

After the run, not during it

These are all on the site and all worth doing, but they're built questions, not real papers, so they don't belong inside the PYQ run:

  • Systems & Theories (119 Q) — the Panwar unit bank with the book's own key. Straight common-section drilling, and the common section is where your gap has always been. Best single follow-up.
  • Gap Diagnostic (60 Q) — sit-once, common-section weighted, compiles your misses into a study sheet on the page.
  • NIMHANS 2025 (59 Q) and 2024 (100 Q) — real verbatim papers, but that cycle closed in June. Content still transfers.
  • Pep mocks, MET mocks, Flight Mock 100 — authored full-length practice. Use for pacing, not for pattern.
  • Practice mode — a question you get right without guessing retires for good; guessed and missed ones stay in rotation. Run it in the gaps between papers.
