RML (GGSIPU) M.Phil Clinical Psychology 2020 — top score & cut-off

You asked for the 2020 topper score and the cut-off. I said RML has never published a topper score and the honest answer might end up being hearsay. I was wrong about that — GGSIPU did publish a full mark-wise merit list for CET code 157 in 2020, every one of the 426 candidates who sat the paper, with obtained marks out of 400. So everything below is official. The archived PDF is linked at the bottom.

Top score: 360 / 400  =  90 questions right out of 100. Two-way tie: Shreya Jha (Outside Delhi) and Gaura Lohani (Outside Delhi). Nobody crossed 90.

Admission cut-off (UR): 344 / 400  =  86 right. That's the lowest CET score among the 11 UR candidates who made the final counselling list. 344 puts you at rank 17–21 out of 426 — top 4.9%.

Paper: 100 MCQs × 4 marks = 400, no negative marking, 2½ hours, held 9 Sept 2020. Because there's no negative marking and every question is worth 4, marks ÷ 4 = questions correct, exactly. 360 means 90 right, 344 means 86 right. That maps straight onto your mock percentages.

OBC(NCL) shows no cut-off because no OBC candidate made the list — 52 OBC candidates sat the paper and the OBC topper (Rabeeza, 336) still didn't appear on the counselling merit list. Read that as "no OBC seat was filled in this cycle", not as a low bar.

The 344 trap — why the cut-off is softer than it looks. Scores clump hard at the top because there's no negative marking and only 4-mark steps. Nine people scored exactly 344. Only two of them got in. Seven at 348, five got in. The written test is 90% of the selection and the interview + practical is 10% — and 10% of 400 is 40 marks, which is ten questions. That's more than the entire spread from 344 to 360. So inside the top band, the interview picks the winners.

Translation: 348 is the score that actually buys safety, 344 is a coin flip, 356+ makes it your seat. Ten candidates who scored 344 or above (including Tanvi Agarwal, rank 3 on 356) do not appear on the final counselling list at all.

Official vs hearsay

Official Everything above. Sources: GGSIPU "Programme wise merit list of M.Phil. (Clinical Psychology), Programme code 157 (appeared candidates)", CET CBT held 09.09.2020, 7 pages, all 426 candidates with obtained marks out of 400 — and GGSIPU Notification 237/2020 dated 08.01.2021, the counselling merit list of 17 for code 157. I cross-matched the 17 names to their CET marks by roll number to get the cut-offs.
Official Exam pattern (100 Q × 4, no negative, 2½ hrs), the 50%/45% qualifying bars, the 1 seat : 3 candidates interview call ratio and the 90% CET / 10% interview + practical weightage — all from the GGSIPU 2020 admission brochure for the programme.
Hearsay Coaching sites (UPS Education, Best Psychology Coaching) float "cut-off 348/400, 87%" as a recent-year figure. It's in the right neighbourhood and it matches the 348 safe band above, but it is not sourced to any GGSIPU document and the year it refers to is vague. Treat it as folklore that happens to be roughly correct, not as a number to plan against.
Hearsay Anything you'll read on Reddit or Quora about "the topper got X" for RML 2020. There is no such claim I could find backed by a document, and now there's no need for one — the real number is 360 and it's printed.
Forum dig — closed I said the forum dig was still open because Reddit was blocking me. I went back at it on 31 July 2026: Reddit's API returns 403 to automated reads, and a full web sweep of Reddit, Quora and the coaching blogs turns up exactly one recurring number — the same "348/400, 87%" line, recycled across UPS Education and Best Psychology Coaching, with no year attached and no document behind it. Nothing new, and nothing sourced. Calling it: the dig is closed. The GGSIPU merit list above outranks every word of it, and it says 360 top, 344 in.
