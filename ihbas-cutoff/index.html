IHBAS M.Phil Clinical Psychology: real top scores and cut-offs

You asked for the top score and the cut-off on your IHBAS 2024 paper. Back on 14 Oct the October 2024 list couldn't be found anywhere readable (only a blurry Scribd copy). It turns out IHBAS still has every list on its own notice board, so everything below is official, read off IHBAS's PDFs on 21 Nov 2026: the October 2024 sitting (your paper), the April 2024 one before it, and the September 2025 one, which comes with the full marks of all 1,647 candidates. Links to every PDF are at the bottom.

Last UR seat: 93. 5 UR seats, and the five were 96, 94, 94, 93, 93. Eight people scored 92 and none got in (seven went to the waiting list).

You got 94 on it (14 Oct, at home, not in an exam hall). On the real list that ties 2nd on the UR list, with only the 96 above you. That's a seat.

How IHBAS marks it (2024-26 and 2025-27 prospectus): 100 MCQs, 90 minutes, no negative marking, no interview. Selection is on the entrance marks alone, so marks = questions right. On the site's mocks, use the number you got right, not the net score (the mocks take off 0.25 a wrong one, IHBAS doesn't).

Highest score in each sitting: Apr 2024 = 90 (three people), Oct 2024 = 96 (one), Sep 2025 = 95 (ten people). Nobody has hit 97 or more in any of the three.

Seats: 11 in Apr 2024 (plus 1 PwD) and Oct 2024. In 2025 RCI raised it to 23 + 1 PwD on 26 Sep 2025, two days before the exam, so UR went from 5 to 11. 2026-28 has 28 seats: UR 11, OBC 7, SC 4, EWS 3, ST 2, PwD 1 (5 Nov 2026 notice). Same 11 UR seats as 2025, more reserved ones.

How ties are broken: your PG %. There's no interview, so when people have the same mark the committee goes by University of Delhi rules, and the 2025 selected list prints how they did it: by overall PG percentage. Seven people scored 94 for the last UR seat. The one with 85.40% got it. Five of the other six went to the waiting list in PG % order (85.10%, 80.80%, 73.00%, 71.70%, 69.97%). Same in OBC (the 90s are ranked by PG %), SC, EWS and ST.

So at the top, one mark is worth a lot. In 2025, 94 was rank 11 to 17 out of 1,647 and 93 was rank 18 to 30.
