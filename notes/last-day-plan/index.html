The three biostats traps to nail (2–3:30)

  • Odds ratio of 1 = no association. OR > 1 = higher odds in the exposed group, OR < 1 = protective. Exactly 1 means the exposure makes no difference.
  • NNT = 1 / ARR. Number needed to treat is one over the absolute risk reduction. If ARR is 0.05 (5%), NNT = 1 / 0.05 = 20. Smaller NNT = better treatment.
  • "Data" is plural. "The data are", not "the data is". A single point is a datum. They love this one as a trick option.
