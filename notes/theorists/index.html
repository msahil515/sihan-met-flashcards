Theorists — who's who, what each one owns, the one-line tell

Last of the four category sweeps. This is the reverse drill: name → what they're famous for. When a stem gives you a person and four contributions (or a contribution and four people), this is the page. Grouped by field. The neighbouring sweeps go the other way (concept → person): Theories, Effects. Built 2026-05-24.

How it's asked. "Who proposed X?", "X is associated with whom?", or "Match the theorist to the concept." The distractor is always a same-field contemporary (Freud's circle, the trait theorists, the attachment crew). Learn each person as a bundle so a single concept pulls the whole name up.

1. Psychoanalytic & neo-Freudian

TheoristWhat they own
Sigmund FreudPsychoanalysis; id/ego/superego; psychosexual stages; defence mechanisms; the unconscious; dream interpretation.
Carl JungAnalytical psychology; collective unconscious; archetypes; introversion/extraversion; coined the Electra complex; word-association.
Alfred AdlerIndividual psychology; inferiority/superiority complex; striving for superiority; style of life; birth order; social interest.
Karen HorneyBasic anxiety; moving toward/against/away from people; womb envy (reply to penis envy); neurotic needs.
Erik Erikson8 psychosocial stages; identity crisis; ego psychology.
Erich FrommEscape from Freedom; character orientations; humanistic psychoanalysis.
Harry Stack SullivanInterpersonal theory; the self-system; good-me/bad-me/not-me.
Melanie KleinObject relations; play therapy; paranoid-schizoid & depressive positions.
Anna FreudChild psychoanalysis; systematised the defence mechanisms.
D. W. WinnicottGood-enough mother; transitional object; holding environment; true/false self.
Heinz KohutSelf psychology; narcissism; empathic mirroring.
Complex ownership: inferiority/superiority = Adler; womb envy = Horney; Electra = Jung (named it), Oedipus = Freud. Don't default everything to Freud.

The high-frequency stats pairings: r & chi-square = Pearson; ANOVA/F = Fisher; t-test = Gosset; alpha = Cronbach; effect size d = Cohen; correlation idea = Galton. These recur every year.
