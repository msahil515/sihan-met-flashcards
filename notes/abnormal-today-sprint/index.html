Block 2, key disorders deep-dive 75 min

Six disorder clusters that show up on every paper. Read each, drill the trap, write the duration / criteria threshold from memory before moving on.

2.1 Mood (cheatsheet §9), 12 min

  • MDD: SIG-E-CAPS, ≥ 5 of 9, must include depressed mood OR anhedonia, ≥ 2 wks.
  • Persistent Depressive (Dysthymia): ≥ 2 yrs (≥ 1 yr in kids).
  • Bipolar I: ≥ 1 manic episode (≥ 1 wk, or any duration if hospitalised). MDE not required.
  • Bipolar II: hypomania (≥ 4 days) + MDE. Never full mania.
  • Cyclothymia: hypomanic + depressive symptoms, never full episode, ≥ 2 yrs.
  • DIG-FAST for mania: Distractibility, Indiscretion, Grandiosity, Flight of ideas, Activity, Sleep ↓, Talkativeness.
TRAP: One full manic episode in their entire history → Bipolar I, even if all current episodes are hypomanic.

2.2 Psychosis (cheatsheet §10), 15 min

  • Schizophrenia: ≥ 2 of (delusions, hallucinations, disorganized speech, grossly disorganized/catatonic behavior, negative sx). At least one must be from the first three. Active ≥ 1 mo, total ≥ 6 mo.
  • Schizophreniform: 1 mo to < 6 mo. Brief Psychotic: 1 day to < 1 mo.
  • Schizoaffective: mood + psychosis, but ≥ 2 wks of psychosis WITHOUT mood symptoms (key differentiator from mood disorder with psychotic features).
  • Delusional Disorder: ≥ 1 mo, delusions only, no other psychotic sx.
  • Bleuler's 4 As: Associations (loose), Affect (flat), Ambivalence, Autism (withdrawal). Note: "autism" here = social withdrawal, NOT autism spectrum.
  • Schneider FRS: thought insertion/withdrawal/broadcasting, audible thoughts, voices commenting/arguing, made impulses, somatic passivity, delusional perception. Suggestive, NOT diagnostic alone in DSM-5.
TRAP: Schizophrenia ≠ DID. Split mind ≠ split personality. Different chapters.
TRAP: Subtypes (paranoid, catatonic, etc.) were dropped in DSM-5. If subtypes appear in answer choices, the question is testing DSM-IV vocab.

2.3 Anxiety / OCD / Trauma (cheatsheet §6, §7, §8), 12 min

  • Anxiety chapter: GAD, Panic, Specific Phobia, Social Anxiety, Agoraphobia, Separation Anxiety, Selective Mutism. Most need ≥ 6 mo.
  • OCD chapter (separated from anxiety in DSM-5): OCD, BDD, Hoarding (NEW), Trichotillomania, Excoriation (NEW).
  • Trauma chapter (separated from anxiety in DSM-5): PTSD, ASD, Adjustment, RAD, DSED. ICD-11 also adds Complex PTSD (DSO: emotion dysregulation, negative self-concept, relational disturbance).
TRAP: OCPD ≠ OCD. OCPD is in the personality disorders chapter, ego-syntonic, perfectionistic. OCD is ego-dystonic obsessions/compulsions.

2.4 Personality Disorders (cheatsheet §14), 12 min

ClusterThemeDisorders
AOdd / eccentricParanoid, Schizoid, Schizotypal
BDramatic / emotional / erraticAntisocial, Borderline, Histrionic, Narcissistic
CAnxious / fearfulAvoidant, Dependent, OCPD
  • Antisocial: disregard for others' rights. Age ≥ 18 + Conduct Disorder before 15. Higher in males.
  • Borderline: instability of relationships, self-image, affect. Impulsivity, fear of abandonment, chronic emptiness, self-harm. Higher in females. First-line: DBT (Linehan).
  • Histrionic: attention-seeking, theatrical, sexually provocative.
  • Narcissistic: grandiosity, need for admiration, lack of empathy.
TRAP: Schizoid (detached, no odd beliefs) vs Schizotypal (detached + odd beliefs / magical thinking). Schizotypal is on the schizophrenia spectrum. Schizoid is not.

2.5 SUD (cheatsheet §15), 8 min

  • DSM-5 collapsed "abuse" + "dependence" into one Substance Use Disorder spectrum.
  • ≥ 2 of 11 criteria in 12 mo. Severity: 2-3 mild, 4-5 moderate, ≥ 6 severe.
  • 4 criterion clusters: impaired control, social impairment, risky use, pharmacological (tolerance, withdrawal).
  • Tolerance/withdrawal from prescribed meds taken as directed do NOT count.

2.6 Eating + Somatic + Dissociative (cheatsheet §11, §12, §13), 8 min

  • Anorexia: low weight required. Bulimia: normal/above weight. BED: binges WITHOUT compensation.
  • Conversion (unconscious, no gain) vs Factitious (conscious, sick role) vs Malingering (conscious, external gain). Malingering is NOT a mental disorder.
  • DID: ≥ 2 distinct identities, recurrent recall gaps, linked to severe early trauma. Renamed from Multiple Personality Disorder.
  • Depersonalization/Derealization: feels unreal, but reality testing INTACT (key differentiator from psychosis).

Q1. A patient has a 2-week history of depressed mood, anhedonia, weight loss, insomnia, fatigue, guilt, and suicidal ideation. Most likely diagnosis?
  1. Persistent Depressive Disorder
  2. Major Depressive Disorder
  3. Bipolar II
  4. Adjustment Disorder with depressed mood

≥ 5 SIG-E-CAPS sx, includes depressed mood AND anhedonia, ≥ 2 wks. PDD needs ≥ 2 yrs.

Q2. Which DSM-5 chapter does OCD live in?
  1. Anxiety Disorders
  2. Trauma- and stressor-related
  3. Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders
  4. Personality Disorders

DSM-5 separated OCD from anxiety. OCPD is in personality disorders, that's the bait.

Q3. Bleuler's 4 As do NOT include:
  1. Associations (loose)
  2. Affect (flat)
  3. Ambivalence
  4. Anhedonia

4th A is Autism (withdrawal). Anhedonia is a negative symptom but not part of Bleuler's set.

Q4. A patient consciously fakes paralysis to avoid jury duty. This is:
  1. Conversion Disorder
  2. Factitious Disorder
  3. Malingering
  4. Somatic Symptom Disorder

Conscious + external gain (avoiding jury duty) = malingering. Not a mental disorder.

Q5. First-line treatment for Borderline Personality Disorder?
  1. CBT
  2. Psychoanalysis
  3. DBT (Linehan)
  4. SSRIs

DBT was developed by Marsha Linehan specifically for BPD. No specific drug; meds are symptom-targeted only.
