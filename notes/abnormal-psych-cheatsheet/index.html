1. Defining Abnormality (4 Ds)

  • Deviance — statistical or social norm violation.
  • Distress — subjective suffering.
  • Dysfunction — impairs daily life / role functioning.
  • Danger — risk to self or others.
TRAP: Deviance alone ≠ abnormal. A genius IQ is statistically deviant but not pathological. Always need at least distress or dysfunction with it.
Some textbooks list a 5th D: Duration, especially for chronicity criteria (≥ 6 months, ≥ 2 years, etc.).

5. Mental Status Exam (MSE), order matters

ABS-MTPCIJ: Appearance, Behavior, Speech, Mood/affect, Thought (form + content), Perception, Cognition, Insight, Judgment.

  • Mood = subjective ("how do you feel?"). Affect = observed (range, congruence, intensity).
  • Thought form = how thoughts flow (loose, tangential, flight of ideas, circumstantial, blocking, perseveration).
  • Thought content = what (delusions, suicidal/homicidal ideation, obsessions, phobias).
  • Perception = hallucinations (auditory, visual, etc.), illusions, depersonalization.
  • Cognition = orientation, attention, memory, abstract thinking.
  • Insight = awareness of being ill. Judgment = decision-making in real situations.

7. OCD & Related Disorders

  • OCD — obsessions (intrusive thoughts) + compulsions (repetitive acts to neutralize). Ego-dystonic. Time-consuming (≥ 1 hr/day) or distressing.
  • Body Dysmorphic Disorder (BDD) — preoccupation with perceived flaw in appearance.
  • Hoarding Disorder — persistent difficulty discarding possessions. New in DSM-5.
  • Trichotillomania — recurrent hair-pulling.
  • Excoriation — recurrent skin-picking. New in DSM-5.
TRAP: OCPD ≠ OCD. OCPD = perfectionist, rigid personality style, ego-syntonic, in the personality disorders chapter. OCD = ego-dystonic obsessions/compulsions, in OCD-related chapter.

8. Trauma- & Stressor-Related

  • PTSD — exposure to actual/threatened death/serious injury/sexual violence + intrusion symptoms (flashbacks, nightmares) + avoidance + negative alterations in cognition/mood + arousal/reactivity. Duration > 1 month.
  • Acute Stress Disorder — same symptom clusters but duration 3 days to 1 month.
  • Adjustment Disorders — emotional/behavioral symptoms within 3 months of stressor; doesn't meet criteria for another disorder.
  • Reactive Attachment Disorder — withdrawn, emotionally inhibited toward caregivers; from severe early neglect.
  • Disinhibited Social Engagement Disorder — overly familiar with strangers; from severe early neglect.
  • ICD-11 adds Complex PTSD — PTSD core + disturbances in self-organization (DSO): emotion dysregulation, negative self-concept, relational disturbance.

10. Schizophrenia & Other Psychotic Disorders

Conceptual history

  • Kraepelin — coined dementia praecox, emphasised early onset + deteriorating course.
  • Bleuler — renamed it schizophrenia, emphasised cognitive fragmentation. 4 As: Associations (loose), Affect (flat), Ambivalence, Autism (withdrawal).
  • Schneider — First-Rank Symptoms (FRS): thought insertion/withdrawal/broadcasting, audible thoughts, voices commenting/arguing, made impulses/actions, somatic passivity, delusional perception. Suggestive but not diagnostic alone in DSM-5.

Symptom domains

  • Positive (added) — hallucinations, delusions, disorganized speech, grossly disorganized/catatonic behavior.
  • Negative (taken away) — flat affect, alogia (poverty of speech), avolition (low motivation), anhedonia, asociality.
  • Cognitive — attention, working memory, executive function deficits.

DSM-5 criteria

  • ≥ 2 of (delusions, hallucinations, disorganized speech, disorganized/catatonic behavior, negative symptoms), at least one from the first three.
  • Active phase ≥ 1 month, total disturbance ≥ 6 months (incl. prodrome / residual).
  • Significant social or occupational dysfunction.
  • Not better explained by mood disorder, substance, or medical condition.

TRAP: Schizophrenia ≠ DID. Schizophrenia = "split mind" (fragmented thought). DID = multiple identities. Different chapters, different mechanisms.
TRAP: DSM-5 removed schizophrenia subtypes (paranoid, catatonic, etc.). They're in DSM-IV, not DSM-5.

11. Dissociative Disorders

  • Dissociative Amnesia — gaps in autobiographical memory, often after trauma. Specifier: with dissociative fugue (sudden travel + identity confusion).
  • Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID) — ≥ 2 distinct personality states, recurrent gaps in recall. Strongly linked to severe early trauma. Renamed from Multiple Personality Disorder.
  • Depersonalization / Derealization Disorder — feeling unreal/detached from self or surroundings, reality testing intact (key differentiator from psychosis).

12. Somatic Symptom & Related

DSM-5 renamed this chapter from "somatoform disorders." Key entries:

  • Somatic Symptom Disorder — distressing somatic symptoms + excessive thoughts/behaviors about them. Symptoms may have a medical basis (focus is on the response).
  • Illness Anxiety Disorder — preoccupation with having illness, few/no somatic symptoms. Replaces hypochondriasis.
  • Conversion Disorder (Functional Neurological Symptom) — neurological symptoms (paralysis, blindness, seizures) without medical explanation. La belle indifférence (oddly calm despite symptom) is a soft sign.
  • Factitious Disorder — intentional faking for sick role gain (no external incentive). Munchausen = on self; Munchausen by proxy = on another (often child).
  • Malingering — conscious faking for external gain (money, avoiding work, drugs). NOT a mental disorder; coded as a V-code / Z-code.
TRAP: Easy to mix up:
  • Conversion = unconscious, no gain. Symptoms are real to the person.
  • Factitious = conscious symptom production, sick role gain (internal psychological).
  • Malingering = conscious, external gain (money, court).

14. Personality Disorders

ClusterThemeDisorders
AOdd / eccentricParanoid, Schizoid, Schizotypal
BDramatic / emotional / erraticAntisocial, Borderline, Histrionic, Narcissistic
CAnxious / fearfulAvoidant, Dependent, Obsessive-Compulsive PD

1-line distinguishers (Cluster B is the most exam-tested)

  • Antisocial — disregard for others' rights, age ≥ 18 + Conduct Disorder before 15. Higher in males.
  • Borderline — instability of relationships, self-image, affect; impulsivity; fear of abandonment; chronic emptiness. Self-harm common. Higher in females.
  • Histrionic — attention-seeking, theatrical, sexually provocative.
  • Narcissistic — grandiosity, need for admiration, lack of empathy.
TRAP: Schizoid vs Schizotypal. Schizoid = detached, indifferent to relationships, no eccentric beliefs. Schizotypal = detached + odd beliefs, magical thinking, perceptual oddities. Schizotypal lies on the schizophrenia spectrum, schizoid does not (in DSM-5).
TRAP: OCPD ≠ OCD. OCPD = ego-syntonic perfectionism. OCD = ego-dystonic obsessions/compulsions.

15. Substance Use Disorder

  • DSM-5 collapsed "abuse" + "dependence" into a single Substance Use Disorder spectrum.
  • Criteria: ≥ 2 of 11 in a 12-month period.
  • Severity: 2-3 mild; 4-5 moderate; ≥ 6 severe.
  • 11 criteria cluster into 4: impaired control, social impairment, risky use, pharmacological (tolerance, withdrawal).
  • Tolerance + withdrawal are pharmacological criteria but not required for diagnosis.
TRAP: Tolerance/withdrawal due to prescribed meds taken as directed do NOT count toward SUD criteria.

16. Sleep-Wake Disorders (compact)

  • Insomnia Disorder — difficulty falling/staying asleep, ≥ 3 nights/wk, ≥ 3 months.
  • Hypersomnolence — excessive sleepiness despite ≥ 7 hr sleep.
  • Narcolepsy — recurrent sleep attacks; cataplexy (sudden muscle tone loss) often present; low CSF hypocretin/orexin.
  • Sleep apnea — obstructive (most common) vs central.
  • Parasomnias — sleepwalking, sleep terrors (NREM); nightmare disorder, REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (REM, often pre-Parkinson's).
  • Circadian rhythm disorders — delayed/advanced sleep phase, shift work type.

17. Childhood / Neurodevelopmental Disorders

  • IDD (Intellectual Developmental Disorder) — onset in dev period; deficits in intellectual + adaptive functioning. Severity by adaptive function, not just IQ.
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) — social communication deficits + restricted/repetitive behaviors. DSM-5 merged Asperger's, PDD-NOS, autistic disorder into one spectrum.
  • ADHD — inattention and/or hyperactivity-impulsivity ≥ 6 months; onset before 12; ≥ 2 settings.
  • Specific Learning Disorder — dyslexia (reading), dysgraphia (writing), dyscalculia (math).
  • Tic Disorders — Tourette's = motor + vocal tics ≥ 1 yr, onset before 18.
  • Communication Disorders — language disorder, speech sound disorder, childhood-onset fluency disorder (stuttering), social (pragmatic) communication disorder.
TRAP: ADHD onset cutoff is before 12 (DSM-5), changed from "before 7" (DSM-IV).
