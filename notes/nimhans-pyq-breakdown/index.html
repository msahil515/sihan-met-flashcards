NIMHANS PYQ Topic Breakdown — 2023 / 2024 / 2025

What this is: A close read of 300 NIMHANS M.Phil Clinical Psych entrance questions across the 2023, 2024, and 2025 memory-based papers. Topics are tagged by repeat frequency so you know where the marks live. Use this to drive revision priority — not the raw textbook order.

Tier legend
  • Tier 1 Repeated in all three years or asked multiple times within a year. Master cold.
  • Tier 2 Repeated in two years. Solid working knowledge.
  • Tier 3 Single appearance. Recognise + classify, don't memorise depth.

1 · Research / Biostatistics

Tier 1 — High repeat, master cold

  • T1 Sampling methods 2023, 2024, 2025: probability vs non-probability, simple random, stratified, cluster (multi-stage), quota, snowball, purposive, convenience. Know calculation of sampling error only for probability methods.
  • T1 Factor analysis basics 2023, 2024: scree plot, eigenvalues, Kaiser-Guttman rule (>1.0), elbow, EFA vs CFA, factor loadings, CFI/RMSEA fit indices.
  • T1 Reliability types 2023, 2024: test-retest (Pearson r), split-half, parallel forms, inter-rater (Cohen's κ), internal consistency (Cronbach α). Know which threat each addresses.
  • T1 Type I vs Type II error 2024, 2025: α (false positive, reject true H0), β (false negative), power = 1−β. Bonferroni correction logic.

Tier 2 — Asked twice across the three years

  • T2 Study designs (cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, RCT) — pick best design for given scenario (rare outcome → case-control; rare exposure → cohort).
  • T2 Reporting guidelines: PRISMA (sys reviews), CONSORT (RCTs), STROBE (observational), CARE (case reports).
  • T2 Parametric vs nonparametric tests: t-test ↔ Mann-Whitney U; paired t ↔ Wilcoxon signed-rank; one-way ANOVA ↔ Kruskal-Wallis; repeated-measures ANOVA ↔ Friedman.
  • T2 Validity types: face, content, construct (convergent + discriminant), criterion (concurrent + predictive).

Tier 3 — Asked once each, recognise + classify

  • T3 Levels of measurement (Stevens: nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio).
  • T3 Z-score interpretation; normal distribution properties (mean=median=mode, AUC=1, kurtosis types: leptokurtic / platykurtic / mesokurtic).
  • T3 df for chi-square (rows−1)(cols−1).
  • T3 Multicollinearity in regression; ANCOVA covariate assumption (linear with DV).
  • T3 Partial correlation, biserial correlation, Spearman rank correlation.
  • T3 Factorial ANOVA for interaction effects.
  • T3 Sociogram (sociometry); ethnographic methodology.
  • T3 Intention-to-treat (ITT) principle; blinding levels.

2 · Neuropsychology / Biological Psychology

Tier 1 — High repeat

  • T1 Aphasias 2024 (multiple Qs): Broca's (non-fluent, intact comprehension, agrammatism), Wernicke's (fluent, poor comprehension, word salad), conduction (arcuate fasciculus), global, anomic, transcortical. Plus angular gyrus = reading/writing.
  • T1 Patient HM (Henry Molaison) 2024 (3+ Qs in one paper): medial temporal lobectomy → anterograde amnesia, intact working memory, intact procedural (mirror tracing). Cornerstone case for hippocampus + memory consolidation.
  • T1 Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome 2024, 2025: thiamine (B1) deficiency in chronic alcohol use. Wernicke = confusion + ataxia + ophthalmoplegia. Korsakoff = anterograde amnesia + confabulation. Confabulation tested separately.
  • T1 Reticular activating system (ARAS) 2023, 2024: brainstem, regulates arousal/wakefulness/attention. Damage → coma.

Tier 2 — Two-year repeats

  • T2 Antipsychotic side effects: tardive dyskinesia (chronic D2 blockade, oro-facial), akathisia (acute restlessness), acute dystonia, neuroleptic malignant syndrome, drug-induced parkinsonism.
  • T2 Memory subtypes: episodic, semantic, procedural; anterograde vs retrograde amnesia; Ribot's law.
  • T2 Endocrine — anterior vs posterior pituitary hormones; adrenal cortex (cortisol, aldosterone) vs medulla (adrenaline/noradrenaline); hypothalamus as integrator; thyroid (metabolism); pineal (melatonin).

Tier 3

  • T3 Genetic disorders: PKU (phenylalanine → tyrosine block), Down (trisomy 21), Turner (XO), Huntington (chromosome 4, autosomal dominant CAG repeat).
  • T3 Neuroimaging: EEG (electrical, ms-temporal, low-spatial), fMRI (BOLD, mm-spatial, ~s temporal), PET, MEG, structural CT/MRI.
  • T3 Lateral ventricles (CSF), choroid plexus, ventricular system flow.
  • T3 Myelin sheath → saltatory conduction (10-100× faster); demyelination → MS.
  • T3 Synaptic plasticity (LTP, LTD); Karl Lashley's distributed memory storage view.
  • T3 Neurodegenerative differential: Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, Huntington's, FTD, vascular dementia, Lewy body.
  • T3 Apraxia, agnosia (incl. prosopagnosia, anosognosia, simultanagnosia), alexia, ataxia — know the difference cold.
  • T3 Action potential mechanics, Na⁺/K⁺/Ca²⁺ channel roles. (See action potential primer.)

3 · Abnormal / Psychopathology

Tier 1 — Master cold

  • T1 Schizophrenia 2023, 2024, 2025: dementia praecox (Kraepelin) → schizophrenia (Bleuler, 4 As). Positive vs negative symptoms (5 As of negative: alogia, anhedonia, avolition, affective flattening, asociality). DSM-5 ≥6 months total / ICD-10 ≥1 month criterion.
  • T1 Capgras-family delusional syndromes 2025 had 4+ Qs: Capgras (familiar = impostor), Fregoli (strangers = one disguised person), Othello (delusional jealousy), De Clérambault's / erotomania, Cotard (nihilistic / "I am dead"), Reduplicative paramnesia. Folie à deux (shared delusion) repeated 2023+2024.
  • T1 Cluster B personality disorders 2023, 2024, 2025: BPD (instability, splitting, abandonment fears), NPD (grandiosity, mirroring failure / Kohut), HPD (theatrical, attention-seeking, seductive), ASPD. Know how to differentiate when stem mentions admiration-seeking vs grandiosity.
  • T1 OCD-related concepts: ego-dystonic vs ego-syntonic, intrusive thoughts, compulsions as safety/avoidance behaviours, Body Dysmorphic Disorder (DSM-5 OCRD chapter), thought-action fusion, magical thinking.

Tier 2

  • T2 Dissociative disorders: DID, dissociative amnesia, fugue, depersonalization/derealization disorder. Trauma origin.
  • T2 Conversion (Functional Neurological Symptom) Disorder + la belle indifférence; differentiating from factitious and malingering.
  • T2 Specific phobia naming: algophobia (pain), nosophobia (disease), cynophobia (dogs), hematophobia (blood), acrophobia (heights), agoraphobia (open/crowded spaces).
  • T2 Mood disorders — DSM-5 specifiers for manic/depressive episodes (with anxious distress, mixed features, psychotic features, peripartum, seasonal). Note: "with limited prosocial emotions" is a Conduct Disorder specifier — common trap.

Tier 3

  • T3 Chronic Fatigue Syndrome / SEID criteria (DSM/ICD).
  • T3 Substance addictive liability ranking (nicotine highest in epi data); gateway theory (marijuana).
  • T3 Eating disorders, somatic symptom disorder, illness anxiety disorder.
  • T3 Treatment of acute mania (lithium, valproate, atypicals; SSRIs NOT first-line).
  • T3 Mental Healthcare Act 2017 provisions (minors, nominated rep, Mental Health Review Board).

4 · Developmental Psychology

Tier 1 — Erikson + Piaget = ~70% of dev questions

  • T1 Erikson's 8 psychosocial stages + virtues 2023, 2024, 2025 (multi-Q each):
    • Trust vs Mistrust (0-1) → Hope
    • Autonomy vs Shame/Doubt (1-3) → Will
    • Initiative vs Guilt (3-6) → Purpose
    • Industry vs Inferiority (6-12) → Competence
    • Identity vs Role Confusion (12-18) → Fidelity
    • Intimacy vs Isolation (18-40) → Love
    • Generativity vs Stagnation (40-65) → Care
    • Ego Integrity vs Despair (65+) → Wisdom
    Memorise the age range AND the virtue. Both have been asked.
  • T1 Piaget's cognitive stages 2023, 2024, 2025:
    • Sensorimotor (0-2): object permanence (substage 4-5), circular reactions.
    • Preoperational (2-7): egocentrism, animism, centration, lack of conservation.
    • Concrete operational (7-11): conservation, classification, decentration; struggles with hypothetico-deductive.
    • Formal operational (11+): abstract reasoning, hypothetical thinking, propositional logic.
    Plus: assimilation vs accommodation, equilibration; moral development (heteronomous → autonomous).
  • T1 Marshmallow Test / delay of gratification 2023, 2024: Mischel preschool paradigm, predicts later self-regulation.

Tier 2

  • T2 Thomas & Chess temperament (NYLS): easy / difficult / slow-to-warm-up; goodness of fit.
  • T2 Bandura observational learning — ARRM (Attention, Retention, Reproduction, Motivation). Reinforcement is NOT required (vicarious is enough).
  • T2 Kohlberg moral development: pre-conventional / conventional / post-conventional, 6 stages.

Tier 3

  • T3 Ainsworth attachment (Strange Situation): A/B/C/D types; Bowlby internal working models.
  • T3 Marcia identity statuses: diffusion, foreclosure, moratorium, achievement (cross of exploration × commitment).
  • T3 Baumrind parenting styles: authoritative > authoritarian / permissive / neglectful (warmth × control).
  • T3 Bronfenbrenner's ecological/bioecological model — micro/meso/exo/macro/chrono + PPCT (covered in primer; not yet a confirmed PYQ but exam-relevant).
  • T3 Vineland Social Maturity Scale — adaptive behaviour assessment.
  • T3 Visual cliff (depth perception in infants); Harlow attachment / contact comfort.

5 · Psychological Assessment

Tier 1

  • T1 Wechsler scales 2024, 2025: WAIS-IV (4 indices: VCI, PRI, WMI, PSI; 3 core subtests each in VCI and PRI), WISC-V; scaled scores M=10 SD=3, composite M=100 SD=15. Letter-Number Sequencing in WAIS but not WISC-V.
  • T1 Indian intelligence batteries 2024, 2025: Bhatia's Battery of Performance Tests (illiterate / rural — performance only), MISIC (Malin), Binet-Kamat / ABKT, Seguin Form Board.
  • T1 Rorschach scoring 2024, 2025: F (Form), FC (Form-Color, with form dominant), CF (Color-Form, with color dominant), C (Pure Color), M (human movement), Cn (Color naming). Major systems: Beck, Klopfer, Piotrowski, Hertz, Rapaport, Exner Comprehensive, R-PAS. Murray = TAT, NOT a Rorschach system.

Tier 2

  • T2 Stanford-Binet ratio IQ (MA/CA × 100) vs Wechsler deviation IQ (M=100 SD=15).
  • T2 Personality inventories: MMPI (objective, empirically keyed, validity scales L/F/K), 16PF (Cattell, factor-analytic), NEO-PI-R (Big Five), MBTI (Jungian types).
  • T2 Projectives: TAT (Murray, ambiguous pictures → narratives), Rorschach, Sentence Completion, Draw-a-Person, Children's Apperception Test.
  • T2 Speed test vs power test distinction.

Tier 3

  • T3 Raven's Progressive Matrices — culture-fair, fluid intelligence.
  • T3 Vineland (adaptive behaviour), DSM/AAIDD intellectual disability levels (IQ 50-70 mild, 35-49 moderate, 20-34 severe, <20 profound).
  • T3 BDI for depression severity; HAM-D / HAM-A.
  • T3 Validity types: discriminant (low correlation with unrelated construct), convergent, predictive, criterion.
  • T3 Allport personality theory — traits as basic units; cardinal / central / secondary.

6 · Psychotherapy / Behaviour Change

Tier 1 — Repeated EVERY year, never miss these

  • T1 Premack Principle 2023, 2024, 2025: high-frequency behaviour reinforces low-frequency one. "Grandma's rule." Asked basically every NIMHANS year.
  • T1 Reinforcement schedules 2023, 2024, 2025 (multi-Q): continuous, FR (high rate, post-rein pause), FI (scallop pattern), VR (highest rate, gambling, most resistant to extinction), VI. Know what each pattern looks like.
  • T1 Operant conditioning quadrants 2023, 2024:
    • Positive reinforcement: add desirable → ↑ behaviour
    • Negative reinforcement: remove aversive → ↑ behaviour
    • Positive punishment: add aversive → ↓ behaviour
    • Negative punishment: remove desirable → ↓ behaviour. Time-out and response cost are negative punishment.
  • T1 Three "schools" with named founders: REBT (Ellis, ABC model, musterbation, irrational beliefs), Cognitive Therapy (Beck, cognitive triad, automatic thoughts, Socratic questioning), Person-Centered (Rogers, three core conditions = UPR + congruence + empathy).

Tier 2

  • T2 Exposure-based therapies: systematic desensitization (Wolpe; relaxation + graded hierarchy), flooding, implosion, prolonged exposure (PTSD), in-vivo vs imaginal.
  • T2 Defense mechanism hierarchy (Vaillant): mature (sublimation, humour, suppression), neurotic (intellectualization, rationalization, repression, reaction formation), immature (acting out, projection, splitting, regression), psychotic (denial, distortion, delusional projection). Splitting = BPD hallmark (Klein, Kernberg).
  • T2 Behavioural techniques: shaping (successive approximations), chaining, fading, prompting, response cost, covert sensitization, habit reversal training (HRT for tics).

Tier 3

  • T3 DBT (Linehan, Zen mindfulness, 4 modules); ACT (Hayes, psychological flexibility); MBCT.
  • T3 Logotherapy (Frankl, will to meaning); Existential therapy (Yalom's 4 ultimate concerns; May; Dasein in Heidegger/Boss/Binswanger; Umwelt/Mitwelt/Eigenwelt).
  • T3 Therapeutic community (Maxwell Jones); milieu therapy; group therapy curative factors (Yalom's 11).
  • T3 Reality therapy (Glasser, 5 needs: survival, love/belonging, power, freedom, fun); Transactional Analysis (Berne, Parent/Adult/Child ego states; injunctions, games).
  • T3 Object relations therapy (Klein, Winnicott transitional objects, Kohut self-psychology with mirroring/idealizing/twinship); Narrative therapy (re-authoring); IPT (4 problem areas).
  • T3 Affirmative therapy (LGBTQ+); ABA (autism, picture exchange).
  • T3 Dodo Bird Verdict — common factors equivalence across therapies.
  • T3 Defensive pessimism vs strategic optimism.

7 · Social Psychology

Tier 1

  • T1 Bystander effect + Kitty Genovese 2023, 2024: Latané & Darley → diffusion of responsibility + pluralistic ignorance.
  • T1 Just-world hypothesis 2023, 2024: Lerner — belief that the world is fair → victim blaming.
  • T1 Type A / Type D personality 2023, 2024: Type A = hostility/competitiveness/time-urgency (CHD risk); Type D = negative affectivity + social inhibition (Denollet, doubles cardiac mortality).

Tier 2

  • T2 Attribution biases: fundamental attribution error (Ross, dispositional over situational), actor-observer asymmetry, self-serving bias, false consensus effect.
  • T2 Cooley's looking-glass self; Mead's symbolic interactionism; self-esteem (affective evaluation) vs self-concept (cognitive description).
  • T2 Halo effect (Thorndike) and other impression-formation biases.

Tier 3

  • T3 Conformity (Asch lines), obedience (Milgram, proximity effects), Sherif Robber's Cave (superordinate goals), social facilitation/loafing/deindividuation.
  • T3 Cognitive dissonance (Festinger & Carlsmith $1 vs $20 study); persuasion (Cialdini's 6 principles; foot-in-the-door / door-in-the-face).
  • T3 Glass ceiling, Matthew effect, Pygmalion / Rosenthal expectancy effects.
  • T3 Reactance (Brehm); learned helplessness (Seligman) → depressive attributional style (internal-stable-global).
  • T3 Anomie (Durkheim); disaffiliation; alienation; group polarization; groupthink (Janis).
  • T3 Tajfel social identity theory; minimal group paradigm; contact hypothesis (Allport's 4 conditions).

8 · General / Cognitive Psychology

Tier 1

  • T1 Memory models 2023, 2024, 2025: Atkinson-Shiffrin (sensory → STM → LTM); Baddeley working memory (central executive, phonological loop, visuospatial sketchpad, episodic buffer added 2000); episodic vs semantic vs procedural; backward digit span = working memory.
  • T1 Defense mechanisms 2024, 2025 (multi-Q): hierarchy (mature/neurotic/immature/psychotic), splitting (Klein/Kernberg), introjection, projection, sublimation, repression, displacement, intellectualization, regression. Linked with personality disorders.

Tier 2

  • T2 Indian psychology: Triguna (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas); Ashtanga Yoga 8 limbs (Yama, Niyama, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi — Karma is NOT a limb).
  • T2 Beck's cognitive triad (self / world / future); cognitive distortions (catastrophising, all-or-nothing, overgeneralization, mind-reading, etc.).
  • T2 Crystallized vs fluid intelligence (Cattell-Horn); Spearman g + s; Sternberg triarchic; Gardner multiple intelligences.
  • T2 Maslow's hierarchy + peak experiences + plateau experiences.

Tier 3

  • T3 Wallas creative process (Preparation → Incubation → Illumination → Verification); insight learning (Köhler chimps).
  • T3 Heuristics: representative, availability, anchoring; algorithm vs heuristic; planning fallacy; thought-action fusion; magical thinking.
  • T3 Carl Jung: collective unconscious, archetypes (Shadow, Anima/Animus, Self, Persona), individuation, synchronicity. Oedipus complex is Freud, not Jung.
  • T3 Cathexis (Freud psychic energy investment); fixation; sublimation.
  • T3 Allport's traits (cardinal/central/secondary); Cattell 16PF + global factors; Eysenck PEN.
  • T3 Perception: Weber's law (ΔI/I = k), Fechner's law (logarithmic); signal detection theory (hit/miss/false-alarm/correct rejection); phi phenomenon; Gestalt principles (Prägnanz, proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, common fate).
  • T3 Lashley distributed memory; Lewin's life space and force-field analysis; Sullivan's interpersonal theory (prototaxic / parataxic / syntaxic modes).

Final priority list (use this for your last 4 weeks)

Week 1-2 — Tier 1 lockdown (~80% of marks)

  1. Erikson's 8 stages + virtues + age ranges
  2. Piaget's 4 stages + key milestones (object permanence, conservation, hypothetico-deductive)
  3. Operant conditioning quadrants + reinforcement schedules + Premack
  4. Three therapy schools (REBT, Beck CT, Rogers PCT) — founder, key concept, technique
  5. Schizophrenia (history, positive/negative symptoms, duration criteria)
  6. Capgras-family delusional syndromes (one card per syndrome)
  7. Cluster B personality disorders + splitting
  8. Aphasias (Broca / Wernicke) + HM + Wernicke-Korsakoff
  9. Memory models (Baddeley + Atkinson-Shiffrin) + episodic/semantic/procedural
  10. Sampling methods + reliability types + Type I/II error
  11. Wechsler scales + Indian batteries + Rorschach scoring
  12. Bystander effect + Just-world + Type A/D

Week 3 — Tier 2 sweep

  • Defense mechanisms hierarchy + splitting + projection
  • Exposure therapies, behavioural techniques (shaping, chaining, response cost, HRT)
  • Dissociative + somatic + conversion disorders
  • Attachment (Ainsworth) + Marcia + Bandura ARRM + Thomas-Chess
  • Antipsychotic side effects (TD, akathisia, NMS)
  • Personality inventories (MMPI vs 16PF vs MBTI vs NEO)
  • Attribution biases + Cooley + halo
  • Beck's cognitive triad; Indian psychology (Triguna, Ashtanga)

Week 4 — Tier 3 recognition + mock papers

  • Run through Tier 3 lists with a recognition-only goal (no deep memorisation; just classify when you see the term).
  • Take NIMHANS Mock 1 (PYQ-based) in one sitting, then debrief every wrong + every guessed.
  • Re-take NIMHANS Mock 1 a week later; gap should narrow.

Watch-outs (NIMHANS specific)

  • "NOT" stems are heavy. ~20% of NIMHANS PYQs ask which is NOT a member of a category. Slow down on those.
  • Year-tagged DSM/ICD criteria. If a stem says ICD-10 → 1 month for schizophrenia. If DSM-5 → 6 months total. Don't conflate.
  • Fictional / clinical-vignette format. NIMHANS leans heavily on case stems (more than MET). Practice extracting the diagnostic core from a paragraph fast.
  • Distractors that swap one well-known concept for another. Capgras vs Fregoli, FAE vs actor-observer, negative reinforcement vs negative punishment, Type A vs Type D. Keep a contrast-pairs list in the relevant cheatsheets.
  • Length of options is no longer a tell on the actual paper. Don't lean on it for guessing — practice on real PYQs.
