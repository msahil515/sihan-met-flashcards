In the hall (decided in advance, not on the day)

  • Skip rule. If a Q takes >90 sec on the first pass, flag it and move on. Come back at the end. Time, not knowledge, is what kills the score.
  • Guess rule (MET +4 / −1). Guess only when you can eliminate at least 2 of 4. Blind guess = −1 expectancy; eliminated-2 guess = +0.5.
  • Don't lean on the longest-option tell. Real MET scrubs the length giveaway. Read all four, decide on content.
  • First instinct. Don't change an answer unless you have a concrete reason. Second-guessing costs more marks than it saves.
  • Two passes. Pass 1: everything you know cold. Pass 2: the flagged ones with whatever time's left.
