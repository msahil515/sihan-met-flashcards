Biopsychology Gaps (3 concrete misses)

Gap 1, Sleep Architecture (Diagnostic Q3 and set-01 Q7 both missed)

Sihan has consistently confused REM and Stage 3 NREM (delta). The discriminating feature is muscle atonia — it happens in REM, not in delta.

Memory hook, “PARADOX”: REM is called “paradoxical sleep” because the brain looks almost awake (fast, mixed-frequency EEG) but the body is paralyzed (atonia). Delta is the opposite — slow brain, normal muscle tone, deep restoration.

Stage EEG Muscles Dreams Notes

NREM 1 Theta (4-8 Hz) Relaxed Hypnagogic images Drowsy, hard to tell asleep
NREM 2 Theta + spindles + K-complexes Relaxed Rare Most of the night is in NREM 2
NREM 3 (delta / slow-wave) Delta (0.5-4 Hz) Normal tone Rare, thought-like Deepest, growth hormone released

MCQ trigger words: - “muscle paralysis” / “atonia” / “body paralysis” → REM - “slow-wave” / “delta” / “deep sleep” → NREM 3 - “sleep spindles” / “K-complexes” → NREM 2 - “paradoxical sleep” → REM - “night terrors” / “sleepwalking” / “bedwetting” → NREM 3 (not REM; these are parasomnias out of deep sleep)

Gap 2, Neuroendocrine — Cortisol Source (Diagnostic Q4 missed)

Sihan picked adrenal medulla for cortisol. Correct is adrenal cortex. These two layers of the adrenal gland do completely different things.

Adrenal anatomy from outside in: - Cortex (outer layer), three zones: - Zona Glomerulosa → Aldosterone (salt/water, mineralocorticoid) - Zona Fasciculata → Cortisol (glucose/stress, glucocorticoid) ★ - Zona Reticularis → Sex steroids (androgens, small amounts) - Medulla (inner layer): - Epinephrine (~80%) and Norepinephrine (~20%) - Modified sympathetic post-ganglionic neurons (chromaffin cells) - Fast response catecholamines, fight-or-flight

Memory hook, “GFR, salt sugar sex”: - G lomerulosa → salt (aldosterone) - F asciculata → sugar (cortisol, raises blood glucose) - R eticularis → sex (androgens) - Medulla = adrenaline (fast, not a steroid)

HPA axis sequence (re-memorize): Stressor → Hypothalamus secretes CRH → Anterior pituitary secretes ACTH → Adrenal cortex (zona fasciculata) secretes cortisol → Negative feedback to hypothalamus + pituitary.

Psychological Assessment Gaps (4 concrete misses)

Gap 1, Predictive vs Concurrent Validity (Q2 missed)

Predictive: criterion measured LATER, in the future. - Example: GRE score today → graduate GPA 2 years from now. - Example: Job aptitude test → job performance 6 months after hire. - Example: Admission test → first-year medical school grades.

Concurrent: criterion measured at the SAME TIME. - Example: New depression scale today vs HAM-D today (comparing to an established gold standard). - Example: IQ test vs teacher rating of intelligence, both this week.

Memory hook: Predictive = Postponed. Concurrent = Current.

Gap 3, TAT Cards (Q4 missed, said 10)

TAT (Thematic Apperception Test): - Creator: Henry Murray with Christiana Morgan, 1935. - Total cards: 31 cards. - Per administration: 20 cards, chosen by age (adult vs child) and sex of subject. - Stimulus: ambiguous black-and-white scenes of people. - Theory: subject projects unconscious needs (Murray’s needs-press theory) onto the ambiguous scene.

Do not confuse with: - Rorschach = 10 inkblots (5 chromatic, 5 achromatic). Herman Rorschach, 1921. - CAT (Children’s Apperception Test) = Bellak, 1949. Animal figures for children.

Memory hook: TAT = Thirty-one, Rorschach = Ten.

Gap 4, KR-20 vs Cohen Kappa (Q5 missed, said kappa)

These are completely different reliability methods. Kappa is NOT internal consistency.

Method What it measures When to use
KR-20 Internal consistency for dichotomous items (right/wrong, true/false) 1 administration, 0/1 scoring. Special case of Cronbach’s α.
Cronbach’s α Internal consistency for Likert/multi-point items 1 administration, multi-point scoring.
Split-half + Spearman-Brown Internal consistency (quick approximation) 1 administration, splits test in half, corrects.
Test-retest Stability across time 2 administrations, 2+ weeks apart.
Alternate forms Equivalence of two versions 2 versions of the test.
Cohen’s kappa Inter-rater agreement on categorical data 2+ raters scoring the same protocols, correcting for chance agreement.
ICC Inter-rater agreement on continuous data 2+ raters, numeric scores.

Kappa asks: “How much do two raters agree on category assignments, beyond chance?” Does not care about items or internal structure.

KR-20 asks: “How consistent are right/wrong items on this one test, with each other?”

If a question says: - “30-item right/wrong exam, one administration, reliability” → KR-20 - “Two clinicians diagnosing the same patients” → Kappa - “21-item Beck Depression Inventory, Likert 0-3, internal consistency” → Cronbach’s α
