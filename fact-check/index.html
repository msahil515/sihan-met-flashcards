Key changes (your score on these can move)

  • RINPAS 2019 Q27: a to c. "Most psychologists believe that aggression is" is Morris and Maisto's own sentence: "largely a learned behavior". So a learned response, not an innate response to frustration.
  • RINPAS 2019 Q78: b to a. The heredity question is Lahey's neuroscience perspective word for word, and Lahey's own practice test keys "neuroscience". Biogenic is the abnormal psych word for biological causes, not a perspective.
  • GMCH 2024 Q81: d to b (now follows GMCH's official key). All four options are Pavlov's own words, so there is no clean answer; the mock goes with GMCH's irradiation and the Key note says so.
  • RML 2024 Q91: c to b (added later on 26 Nov). "The visual field covered by each human eye" is about 160 degrees: 60 towards the nose, 100 to 107 towards the temple. 180 degrees is the figure for both eyes together, so GGSIPU's printed key was wrong.
  • Amity 2023 Q80: a to c (added 27 Nov). The token economy as a therapy was built by Teodoro Ayllon and Nathan Azrin (Anna State Hospital ward from 1961, paper 1965, book 1968). Skinner only gave the operant principles behind it, so the coaching book's key (Skinner) was wrong. The Final Textbook already teaches it as Ayllon and Azrin.
  • Psych Entrance Mock 2 Q107: a to d (added 27 Nov). This is the site's own practice mock, so the key was ours, and it was backwards. In the scheme the question comes from (Angie Titchen and Dawn Hobson, the phenomenology chapter in Somekh and Lewin's Research Methods in the Social Sciences, 2005), the direct approach (phenomenological sociology) uses an uninvolved, detached observer and the indirect approach (existential phenomenology) an involved, connected one. So the answer is "direct; indirect".
  • Systems & Theories Q66, Q88 and Q112: c to b (added 27 Nov). The questions come from a coaching bank, but these keys were ours, and all three were wrong. Q66: expectancy, demand and incentive is how Luthans' Organizational Behavior describes the cognitive framework (Tolman), and the Panwar key itself says cognitive, so not social learning. Q88: studying how psychological processes differ among people of different cultures is the textbook definition of cross-cultural psychologists, not social psychologists. Q112: Mysore started its psychology department in 1924, two years before the Indian Journal of Psychology (1926), so the order is Calcutta, Mysore, Journal, Madras, Pune (option b).
  • IMHANS 2021 Key notes corrected, keys the same. LBS's revised key did not switch Q15 and Q72 to a. It accepts two answers (Q15 a or c, Q72 a or b, Q51 b or c), so the mock's answers were accepted too. 2021 is now 74 of 75 against LBS.

Every other paper: no key changes. GMCH 2023 and 2025 matched 100 of 100 on a blind re-solve; the IMHANS keys already follow the official LBS keys, so only the explanations were checked.
