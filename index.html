How NIMHANS differs from MET. Same psychology base, very different exam. No language/English section, no +1/−0.25 high-stakes scoring. It is broader and more factual: theorist-to-concept, test-to-subtest, therapy-to-founder, brain-area-to-function, DSM/ICD detail, and research-design recall. The four heaviest baskets historically are Research & Statistics, Psychotherapy, Assessment/Psychometrics and Biopsychology.
