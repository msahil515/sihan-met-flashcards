How long will the complete notes take?

Straight answer for learning it cold, not skimming: roughly 5 to 6 weeks at 3 to 4 hours a day for a proper first pass that actually sticks. That is the full set at depth, around a hundred chapters across the core texts, regrouped into the nine syllabus areas below. Three ways to pace it, then the smarter route.

Learning it cold

5–6 weeks

At 3–4 hrs/day. A genuine first pass where it sticks, not skimming. About 130–150 hrs total.

Compressed

~1 month

Push to 6 hrs/day and the same first pass folds into roughly four weeks. Heavier days, less calendar.

Just revising

~2 weeks

If you already know chunks and you are only refreshing, it is far faster. Reading to remind, not to learn.

Don't read it cover to cover. That is the slow play. Smarter move: let the PYQ priorities point you at the high-yield clusters first, hit those hard, then backfill the rest. You get exam-ready long before you have touched every chapter. The breakdown below is ordered heaviest-first for exactly that reason.

A realistic 6-week first pass

Cold, at 3–4 hrs/day, heaviest baskets front-loaded so you are exam-useful early. Read the callout above first.

Week 1
Research & Statistics, all of it, then start Psychotherapy. Designs, sampling, reliability/validity, the test families, ethics. NIMHANS's single heaviest basket, so it goes first.
Week 2
Finish Psychotherapy, then start Assessment. School-by-school, founder pairs, technique-to-school. Then into the test-to-subtest grind.
Week 3
Finish Assessment, then start Biopsychology. Wechsler/Stanford-Binet/Raven's, MMPI/16PF/NEO, Rorschach/TAT. Then neurons, anatomy, NTs, HPA.
Week 4
Finish Biopsychology, then Abnormal / Clinical. Action potential, sleep, cranial nerves. Then DSM-5 vs ICD-11, the disorder groups, diagnostic detail.
Week 5
Cognitive, then Developmental. Learning theories, memory models, attention phenomena. Piaget/Erikson/Kohlberg, attachment, Vygotsky, Bronfenbrenner.
Week 6
Social, Personality, then full review. Conformity, attribution, group processes. Trait/psychodynamic/humanistic/social-cognitive. Then straight into mocks.
