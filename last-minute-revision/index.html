SECTION 1
Therapies & founders

  • Beck = CBT, cognitive triad (negative view of self, world, future). Distortions: overgeneralization, catastrophizing, black-and-white thinking.
  • Ellis = REBT, ABC model (Activating event, Belief, Consequence). Dispute irrational beliefs.
  • Wolpe = systematic desensitization (relaxation + a fear hierarchy). Flooding = full intensity, no hierarchy.
  • Rogers = client-centered (unconditional positive regard, empathy, congruence).
  • Perls = Gestalt (here and now, empty chair). Frankl = logotherapy + paradoxical intention. Berne = transactional analysis.
  • Linehan = DBT (borderline). Klerman = IPT. Kabat-Zinn = MBSR. Haley = strategic (prescribing the symptom).
  • Token economy = operant. Aversion therapy, modeling = Bandura.

SECTION 2
Defense mechanisms (Vaillant hierarchy)

Psychotic (denial, distortion) < immature (projection, acting out, regression) < neurotic (repression, reaction formation, displacement, rationalization) < mature (sublimation, humor, altruism, suppression).

Low to high: psychotic at the bottom, mature at the top. Sublimation / humor / altruism are the mature ones; projection / acting out are immature.

SECTION 3
Conditioning

  • Classical = involuntary reflex (Pavlov). Operant = voluntary, driven by consequences (Skinner).
  • Negative reinforcement = remove something bad to increase a behavior. NOT punishment.
  • Reinforcement schedules: variable ratio = most resistant to extinction.

SECTION 4
Personality theorists

Freud (psychosexual, id/ego/superego), Erikson (8 psychosocial stages), Jung (collective unconscious, archetypes), Adler (inferiority, striving for superiority), Bandura (self-efficacy), Maslow (hierarchy of needs), Allport (traits), Cattell (16PF), Eysenck (E, N, P), Big Five = OCEAN.

SECTION 5
Developmental

  • Piaget: sensorimotor, preoperational, concrete operational, formal operational.
  • Erikson: trust vs mistrust → integrity vs despair (8 stages).
  • Kohlberg: preconventional, conventional, postconventional.

SECTION 6
Stats & research

  • df = n minus 1. Mean shifts with outliers, median doesn't.
  • Reliability = consistency (test-retest, split-half, inter-rater, Cronbach's alpha). Validity = accuracy (content, construct, criterion = concurrent + predictive).
  • Type I = false positive (reject a true null) = alpha. Type II = false negative = beta. Power = 1 minus beta, rises with bigger n.
  • Pearson = interval data. Spearman = ranked/ordinal. Parametric needs normality + interval; non-parametric (Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon, Kruskal-Wallis, chi-square) don't.
  • Factor analysis = data reduction. Kaiser = eigenvalue over 1.

SECTION 7
Testing

MMPI = objective. Rorschach + TAT = projective. WAIS = adult IQ, WISC = kids, Stanford-Binet too.

SECTION 8
Biopsych

Neurotransmitters: dopamine (reward/movement, low in Parkinson's, high in schizophrenia), serotonin (mood), GABA (main inhibitory), glutamate (main excitatory), acetylcholine (memory, low in Alzheimer's).

Broca = speech production (frontal). Wernicke = comprehension (temporal). Sympathetic = fight or flight, parasympathetic = rest and digest.

SECTION 9
Clinical / ICD-11

Dropped the old neurosis categories. Added gaming disorder, complex PTSD; hoarding now separate from OCD; gender incongruence moved out of mental disorders.

Positive symptoms (hallucinations, delusions) vs negative (flat affect, avolition, anhedonia). Prodromal = early warning phase.

SECTION 10
Indian psych — drill this, it's your gap

8 limbs of yoga: yama, niyama, asana, pranayama, pratyahara (sense withdrawal), dharana (one-point focus / directing energy to one point), dhyana (sustained meditation), samadhi.

Gunas: sattva (purity), rajas (activity), tamas (inertia).

Kleshas = 5 afflictions, avidya is the root. Chitta vritti = mental fluctuations, yoga stills them.
