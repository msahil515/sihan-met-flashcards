Correction 1: Internet Archive does not lend the Trull and Phares 6th edition. I said it did. Correction 2: on the Oxford Handbook, Lopez is listed first. I said Snyder was first. That is only true for the 2002 first edition and the third edition. The title in your forward, Oxford Handbook of Positive Psychology in the Oxford Library of Psychology series, is the 2009 second edition, and on that one it is Lopez and Snyder. So that citation was closer than I made it sound: the year is wrong and Snyder is missing, nothing else.

The four books

1

Pomerantz, Clinical Psychology Library or buy

Pomerantz, A. M. (2008). Clinical psychology: Science, practice, and culture. Sage Publications. ISBN 978-1-4129-3752-8.

Search: "Pomerantz Clinical Psychology Science Practice". From the later editions the subtitle changed to Science, Practice, and Diversity, so if you search the old title only you will miss the new ones. The current one is the 6th edition (Sage, 2023, ISBN 978-1-0719-1056-6). Any edition is fine for a foundations course.

Internet Archive: the 2008 and 2011 copies are print-disabled only, so no loan. Free: Sage runs a free student site for the 6th edition.

2

Trull and Phares, Clinical Psychology 5th ed free to borrow

Trull, T. J., & Phares, E. J. (2001). Clinical psychology: Concepts, methods, and profession (6th ed.). Wadsworth/Thomson Learning. ISBN 0-534-54856-3.

Search: "Trull Phares Clinical Psychology Concepts Methods Profession". Borrow instead: Phares, E. J., & Trull, T. J. (1997). Clinical psychology: Concepts, methods, and profession (5th ed.). Brooks/Cole. ISBN 0-534-26298-8. That is the one on Internet Archive you can actually open.

Newer: the book carried on as Trull and Prinstein, and the current one is Abramowitz, Prinstein and Trull, Clinical Psychology, 9th edition (Worth, 2023). If your college library has a recent copy on the shelf, that is the one to pick up.

3

Lopez and Snyder, The Oxford Handbook of Positive Psychology 1st ed free to borrow

Lopez, S. J., & Snyder, C. R. (Eds.). (2009). The Oxford handbook of positive psychology (2nd ed.). Oxford University Press. ISBN 978-0-19-518724-3.

Search: "Oxford Handbook of Positive Psychology". Borrow instead: Snyder, C. R., & Lopez, S. J. (Eds.). (2002). Handbook of positive psychology. Oxford University Press. ISBN 0-19-513533-4. There is also a third edition (Snyder, Lopez, Edwards and Marques, ISBN 978-0-19-939651-1).

How to read it: it is an edited handbook, dozens of chapters by different authors, not a textbook. Nobody reads it cover to cover. Your course will point to particular chapters (hope, optimism, flow, resilience and so on); read those.

Baumgardner and Crothers, Positive Psychology Buy, cheap

Baumgardner, S. R., & Crothers, M. K. Positive psychology. Pearson Education India. ISBN 978-93-325-3792-7.

Search: "Baumgardner Crothers Positive Psychology". "Steve, B." in your forward is Steve Baumgardner with his first name and surname swapped, and Marie Crothers left out. Search "Steve B" and you get nothing.

Internet Archive: not there. Buy: the Pearson India paperback, listed at about Rs 462 on Amazon.in when I searched (prices move). While it ships: Compton, An Introduction to Positive Psychology (2005) is an undergraduate positive psychology text you can borrow on Internet Archive now.

Borrowing on Internet Archive

Make a free account at archive.org/account/signup, open the book link above, and press Borrow. The book reads in the browser; there is no PDF download, and it goes back automatically when the loan ends.

The copy counts on this page (13, 3 and 1 free) are what the archive showed on 13 September. If all copies are out, there is a waitlist button on the same page.
