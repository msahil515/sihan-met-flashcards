Straight answer:

1. JSS Mysuru: no paper exists online, and no recall either. JSS makes you hand the booklet back and bans taking questions out, so nothing leaks. What JSS does print: 2025 M.Phil was 90 MCQs in 60 minutes, no negative marking, interview 10 marks; 2026 M.A. and PDCP was one 1-hour MCQ paper, no negative marking, plus a viva (no question count given).

2. SGT Gurugram: no written paper or recall exists online. SGT also takes the booklet back. What is out there: interview recalls only. 2026 M.A. (Reddit): dopamine pathways, what you would do if someone had a panic attack in front of you, an example of formal thought disorder; written paper called easy to moderate, interview trickier. 2025 M.Phil (UPS video): internship, the MMPI, comorbidities and their treatment, your favourite school of thought, Carl Rogers.
