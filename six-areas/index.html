Six areas, one syllabus

The whole prep for IHBAS, CIP Ranchi, JSS Mysuru and DIMHANS Dharwad on one page. Built 28 July 2026. Seven blocks, once clinical is counted properly. Tick one off when you've read it and drilled it; the ticks save on this device.

Three things before you start

1. These are not four syllabi. They're one. IHBAS, CIP, JSS and DIMHANS are all general-psychology MCQ papers built out of roughly the same areas. You study once and it counts for all four. Prepping them separately is how you burn three months doing the same reading three times.

2. Reading without testing is where people lose months. One topic read properly, then 40 to 50 questions on that same topic the same day, then read every explanation, including the ones you got right. That's the loop. Everything below is built to feed it.

3. IHBAS has no negative marking. You attempt all 100, you never leave a blank. The mocks on this site are scored +1 / −0.25 so they stay comparable to each other, so an IHBAS mock will read lower here than the real paper would. On IHBAS papers, count attempted-and-right out of 100 and ignore the penalty.

One correction to what I sent you on WhatsApp. I said abnormal and clinical sits on top for CIP and JSS but not IHBAS. That's wrong, and it's the kind of wrong that costs you a paper, so here it is properly.

IHBAS publishes a fixed 100-question blueprint and it reads: Clinical Psychology 25, Abnormal Psychology 15, then ten each for General, Social, Physiological, Personality, Developmental and Statistics. That's 40 of 100 clinical and abnormal on your top target. And it isn't just the blueprint: every question of the three real IHBAS papers on this site (2020, 2024, 2025, 292 questions) was tagged and counted, and the clinical-plus-abnormal block lands at 33 to 38 every single year. The blueprint holds.

So the order below is the one I gave you with abnormal and clinical moved into second place, where the marks actually are. Everything else stands. The full blueprint and the tagged counts are on the syllabus hub →

Still first, and the correction below doesn't move it. It's 10 questions on the IHBAS blueprint and 9 on the real CIP 2020 paper, so it isn't the biggest block, but it is the only area you can learn cold: it doesn't depend on how much you remember from BA, the content doesn't move, and it repeats every single year in every one of the four papers. Watch for: the exams love the small mechanical stuff, not the theory. Which test for which design, what df equals, what a scale of measurement permits, what happens to a correlation when you restrict range. Learn to answer those without thinking.

SocialNAME-HEAVY

Sixth because it's mostly named studies and named effects, which is fast to revise but easy to confuse under time pressure. Asch, Milgram, Sherif, Festinger, Zimbardo, Latané and Darley: the exams ask for the finding and the name together. It's rewarding to revise late because it stays fresh.

Last, and that is not the same as least. The daily shape

Don't overthink it. One pass through this three times a day is the whole method, and the third box is the one everybody skips.

Step 1

Read one topic properly

One topic, from the notes above, read like you intend to be asked about it. Not the whole area, not three topics. One. If it takes 90 minutes, it takes 90 minutes.

Step 2

40 to 50 questions, same topic, same day

Same day is not a detail, it's the point. The questions are what convert reading into recall. Pull them from the drill links on that area's card.

Step 3 · the whole thing

Read every explanation

Including the ones you got right. A right answer you guessed is a miss you didn't get charged for, and it will come back as a real miss in the exam. Every mock here prints the full explanation and a per-option trap line, so this is reading, not hunting.

Which paper, and when

The reading order above tells you what to study. The paper order tells you when to test it. They're separate, and you run them at the same time.

IHBAS 2024 cold, first. Before you sink a fortnight into any one of the six areas, sit the one paper you own that is real, verbatim, and carries the institute's own answer key. Full 100, no notes, one sitting. It tells you which of the six areas is leaking, so you don't spend three weeks perfecting the area that was already fine.

Then IHBAS 2025 (98 of 100 now matched to the official key, Q77 and Q86 still disputed), then IHBAS 2020 back to back with the other two so you can see what IHBAS actually repeats. Then CIP 2020 once you give me the go, it's queue #1 and the source pack prints full worked answers, so it comes out exact.

How you'll know which area is leaking

You don't guess at this, and you don't go by feel. Every attempt you log on this device gets joined back to the topic of each question, and the Gap Map ranks your leaks across every mock you've sat. Sit IHBAS 2024, open the gap map, and the top of that list is where the next fortnight goes, whatever this page says the order is. The order below is the default when you have no data.
