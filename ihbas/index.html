IHBAS entrance: the last 4 days

Master in Clinical Psychology 2026-28, Institute of Human Behaviour and Allied Sciences, Delhi (University of Delhi, RCI approved). Entrance Sun 29 Nov 2026, fixed by the DU Examination Branch letter of 23 Nov. Admit card out 26 Nov: paper 10:00 to 11:30 AM, report 9:00, gate shuts 9:45, at Kirori Mal or Hansraj College, DU North Campus, by roll number (section 5). Built Wed 25 Nov 2026, the evening after St John's. This is for you if your form reached IHBAS by 21 Nov.

Your name is not on IHBAS's eligible list. IHBAS posted the list of candidates who can sit on 26 Nov, then an updated one on 27 Nov: 1,543 names, roll numbers 260001 to 261543, 45 pages. The update added 7 late names (261537 to 261543) and fixed 18 categories and spellings. Every row of both lists was read and no candidate name has Sihan in it. The admit card has to carry your roll number from that list, so without one there is no way in on Sunday. If your form did reach IHBAS, the only address in their exam notices is ihbasmphil.appl@delhi.gov.in, and their list says spelling mistakes get fixed at counselling, so a misspelt name would still be on it. The updated list (PDF).
Paper 100 MCQs, 4 options, 90 min Negative marking none Interview none, marks alone Seats 28 (UR 11) Ties broken on PG %
The short version. Two full IHBAS-length papers on a 90-minute timer, Thursday and Friday, each one reviewed the same day. Saturday morning goes on what both of them caught you on, and nothing new after that. On Sunday every one of the 100 gets an answer, because a blank scores the same as a wrong one. Last year the last UR seat went at 94 right, and seven people were on 94 for it (section 3).

  • 2026-28 has 28 seats: UR 11, OBC 7, SC 4, EWS 3, ST 2, PwD 1 (5 Nov notice). Same 11 UR seats as 2025, more reserved ones.
  • Nobody has scored 97 or more in any of the last three sittings. In 2025, 94 was rank 11 to 17 of 1,647 and 93 was rank 18 to 30, so one question is a lot of places.
  • There is no interview, so a tie goes on overall PG %. That is already fixed, so marks are the only lever left.

4 · 90 minutes, 100 questions

ThingHow
Pace54 seconds a question on average. If one will not come in that time, mark it and move on.
BlanksNever. No negative marking, so a guess can only add. Down to two options: pick one, mark it for later, move on.
Pass 1, about 60 minEverything you know, at speed. Mark the ones you guessed.
Pass 2, about 20 minBack to the marked ones only.
Last 10 minCheck every one of the 100 has an answer marked. Change an answer only if you can say why the first one was wrong.
On the mocksSit Mocks 1 and 2 with this same pass 1, pass 2, last 10 min routine.

5 · The day and after

ThingWhat
TimeSun 29 Nov 2026, 10:00 to 11:30 AM. Report at 9:00 AM. Nobody gets in after 9:45 AM. You cannot leave the room before the paper ends.
CentreBy roll number, both on DU North Campus, Delhi 110007:
260001 to 260800: Kirori Mal College
260801 to 261543: Hansraj College
Nearest metro: Vishwavidyalaya (Yellow Line). No parking at the centres.
Admit cardNot a ready-made card. It is a blank form in IHBAS's instructions PDF (pages 2 to 4, one per centre): print it, fill in your roll number from the eligible list, name, father's name, category (added in the 27 Nov version), address, phone and email, paste the same photo you put on the application form, and sign it. DU staff check it at the centre.
BringThe filled admit card (original) plus a photocopy of it, one original photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID or Driving Licence) plus a photocopy of it, and a blue or black ball pen. PwD candidates: the original disability certificate. Not allowed in the hall: phone, calculator, books, notes, any electronic gadget.
In the hallAnswers go on an OMR sheet in blue or black ball pen. Do rough work only on the question paper: any rough work on the OMR sheet and it is not marked. Hand in the OMR sheet and the admit card before you leave. You keep the question booklet.
Answer keyProvisional key on the IHBAS site the same day. To challenge one: Rs 200 a question, by email to ihbasmphil.appl@delhi.gov.in, by 1 Dec 2026, 2 PM. Result goes on the final key, no grievances after it. Your own OMR copy: Rs 1,000 within a week of the exam.
SaturdayCentre and route checked, and how long it takes at that hour on a Sunday. IHBAS pays no travel or stay, and you arrange your own.
ResultIHBAS puts the counselling list (with marks) and the selected and waiting lists on its notice board. In Oct 2024 the exam was 20 Oct and the selected list came 28 Oct. In 2025 the exam was 28 Sep and the selected list 15 Oct. No individual intimation to anyone not selected.
If selectedCounselling and document verification on the date IHBAS notifies. Every claim in your form needs its document. At joining: the migration certificate from your university and a contract bond on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper (stipend ₹25,000 a month). Join and pay by the date in the selection letter, or the seat goes to the waiting list.

Check yourself: the 2026-28 prospectus if it goes up before Sunday, in case the pattern changed (the instructions say nothing about negative marking).
