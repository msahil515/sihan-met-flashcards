Phobia buzzer · best of 10

Definition drops on screen. First player to type the phobia name and hit Enter scores the round. Wrong guess locks you out for that round, the other player can still grab it. 10 rounds, highest score wins. Tie goes to sudden death.

Get ready

One device, two players, two text boxes. Sit side by side, claim a panel, tap your box when the round opens, type the phobia, hit Enter.

  1. Tap your box first. P1 is left, P2 is right (top / bottom on phone).
  2. Read the definition. Type the phobia name (e.g. arachnophobia).
  3. Press Enter to lock in. First correct lock-in wins the round.
  4. Wrong guess? Your box greys out for the round. Opponent still has a shot.
  5. 10 rounds. Highest score wins. Tie = single sudden death round.
