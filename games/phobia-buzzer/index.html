Phobia buzzer · 4-option MCQ · best of 10

Definition drops. Both players see the same 4 options A/B/C/D. First to tap the right one scores the round. Tap a wrong option and you're locked out for the round. 10 rounds, highest score wins. Tie = sudden death.

Get ready

One device, two players, two button grids. Sit side by side, claim a panel, tap your answer when the round opens.

  1. P1 panel left, P2 right (top / bottom on phone). Each panel has its own 4 buttons.
  2. Read the definition. Same 4 options A–D show on both sides, same order.
  3. Tap to lock in. First correct tap wins the round.
  4. Wrong tap? Your panel greys out for the round. Opponent still has a shot.
  5. 10 rounds. Highest score wins. Tie = single sudden death round.
