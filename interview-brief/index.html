Completed dissertation: very short revision card

2024 study

Variables. Big Five traits (BFI-10), aggression (BPAQ-SF), sexual compulsivity (SCS), and pornography ever-use, frequency and perceived control; age and sex were also recorded.

Original hypotheses, in brief. H1 males report more frequent use; H2 greater compulsivity relates to greater frequency; H3 higher conscientiousness relates to greater perceived control; H4 lower neuroticism and agreeableness relate to a higher likelihood of ever-use; H5 compulsivity and personality relate to control; H6 higher aggression relates to more “problematic” use; H7 aggression relates positively to compulsivity among consumers.

Reported results. In the regression tables, compulsivity and neuroticism were significant for ever-use; compulsivity alone for frequency; conscientiousness, compulsivity and aggression for control. Aggression and compulsivity correlated positively in the full sample. Other model coefficients were nonsignificant.

Cross-sectional study, N=214: these are associations, not causes. The displayed sex-by-ever-use result does not establish the sex-frequency analysis for H1. The full-sample correlation does not establish H7's consumer-only association. Today's proposed hypotheses are revised wording, not the original tested set.
